# Rules for agents maintaining this site

This site is an unofficial wiki for the game Neverness to Everness (NTE, 異環). Its first readers are AI agents; humans come second. Markdown source is the only content source, and the HTML is a skin over it. The rules in `../AGENTS.md` apply; this file holds what is specific to this site.

## Decisions by the owner

- Languages: English, Traditional Chinese (`/zh-tw/`), Simplified Chinese (`/zh-cn/`, added by the owner on 2026-11-18), Japanese (`/ja/`) and Korean (`/ko/`, both added by the owner on 2026-11-22). The Japanese and Korean pages are translated from the English pages by delegates, by the owner's choice.
- Scope of the first release: 35 pages. Home, one page per version (1.0 to 1.5), one page per playable character (25), two system pages (Boards, platforms) and the names page.
- Second release (2026-11-19): six system pages (Esper Cycles, Arcs, Consoles, progression materials, vehicles, properties) and Growth and Awakenings sections on every character page. Prefer facts that two sites share; leave out time-limited facts.
- Third release (owner, 2026-11-21): two system pages, Combat formulas (`systems/combat`) and Sea Angler and Bagel (`systems/sea-angler-bagel`), a public transport section on the vehicles page, and sections on Console dismantling and on whose stats an Esper Cycle uses. Player research from the NGA forum and from the Bilibili channel 零号攻略组 may serve as sources; a fact found in only one of them is attributed in the sentence. An endgame modes page waits for a change to how the home page reaches the system pages.
- Skill multipliers (owner, 2026-11-22): every character page gets a last section with the per-level values of its four skills from the game data 1.5.4; character pages now list their nanoka.cc character files as sources.
- Game data: the owner accepted the versioned game data that nanoka.cc publishes (`static.nanoka.cc/nte/<version>/`, English and Simplified Chinese) as the source of in-game text: names, Arc stats and effects, Cartridge sets, Awakenings, base stats, materials, vehicles, properties. Only released content goes in (Exe and the Arc "Dotted Horizon" were in 1.5.4 data before release). Check samples against mysqil, Everness and the official notices, take a bounded number of files, and do not crawl the sites: nanoka.cc and everness.info carry `ai-train=no` and block AI crawlers.
- Story text (owner, 2026-11-23): character archives (`profile_detail`), outfit descriptions, and the descriptions of Arcs (on the Arcs page) and of the materials on the progression page are shown as the game writes them, in each language's own edition: English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean from the nanoka.cc files, Traditional Chinese from everness.info, whose text is the Taiwan localisation (it differs in wording from the Simplified text). Never converted with OpenCC, never paraphrased. Archive pages sit at `characters/<slug>/archive`, linked from the Profile table and the character list. They are hidden from the autogenerated sidebar; on a character's page and on its archive page, `src/routeData.ts` adds the archive as a child row under the character, labelled with the archive page's title (owner, 2026-11-24). They carry `hideAnswer: true`: no visible answer line, the description goes into the hidden note. A character whose archive the game has not opened (one entry, "Profile locked": Esper Zero in 1.5.4) gets no archive page. Outfit rows that name several outfits put each name before its text. As with art, remove text when the publisher asks. Anomaly files come from the nanoka.cc monster files, not from GameKee's player transcriptions: GameKee's user agreement (4.5) forbids copying, reposting and compiling its content without written permission.
- Official character introductions (owner, 2026-11-24): the facts in them (post, team, workplace, named weapon or companion) go in a Role row of the Profile table, in each language's wording; the descriptions of character and manner are left out. They are not rewritten in our own words, and no sentence reports what the introduction says.
- Voice lines (owner, 2026-11-25): each character's voice lines (`voices` in the nanoka.cc character files: daily and combat) are shown as the game writes them, in each language's own edition, as the archive entries are: English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean from the nanoka.cc files, Traditional Chinese from the voice tab of the character's everness.info page. One page per character, `characters/<slug>/voice`.
- Anomaly stories and enemy manual entries (owner, 2026-11-25): no Traditional Chinese source has them, so their Traditional Chinese text is made from the Simplified game text until one does; then it is replaced by that source's text. This is the one exception to "never converted" above. The game's Taiwan localisation differs from the Simplified text mostly in vocabulary: of 258 archive, outfit and Arc texts, 95 matched the Taiwan text exactly after OpenCC `s2twp`, and the median similarity was 99.3%.
- The anomaly list (owner, 2026-11-26) has one row per anomaly commission in the nanoka.cc monster list (a `Vision` entry, whose type `Delete` means the player defeats its monster; 異象委託 in the game's Chinese), those whose anomaly has no archive file included. Other commissions (「C的魔方」, 「赤門」 on everness.info's achievements page) have no monster entry and are not listed; each anomaly page names its commissions. A commission's name, code and difficulty are game text from the file's `VisionName`, `VisionCode` and `DifficultyDisplayText`.
- Item and achievement lists (owner, 2026-11-26): glider skins, mod parts, figurines, gifts and achievements, one list page each under `items/`, show the game's names and descriptions as the game writes them, in each language's own edition. Traditional Chinese is everness.info's Taiwan text where it has the entry (item pages, and the achievements that its achievements page shows); otherwise it is made from the Simplified game text, as for the anomaly stories, until a Traditional source has it (in 1.5.4: every listed item has a Taiwan page; the figurines' Source column and 430 of the 459 achievements are converted). Agent choices (2026-11-26): mod parts whose English, Japanese or Korean text in the nanoka.cc files is still the Simplified original are left out (42 of 58 in 1.5.4); dishes that characters prefer are not in `item.json` and are not on the gifts page; achievement rewards are left out, their unit being unknown.
- Game text is written into the pages by script from records (owner, 2026-11-23): `pipeline/` fetches a bounded list of files, writes `records/characters/<slug>.json`, `records/voices/<slug>.json`, `records/arcs.json`, `records/items.json`, `records/anomalies.json` and `records/lists.json`, and writes the archive and voice line pages, the pages under `anomalies/` (the list of anomaly commissions, one page per anomaly file and the enemy manual; `pipeline/anomaly_pages.py`), the item and achievement lists under `items/` (`pipeline/list_pages.py`), the Outfits description column, the Profile rows, the lists of archive and voice line pages (`characters/archives`, `characters/voices`; `pipeline/character_lists.py`) and the description tables at the end of the Arcs and progression pages. Do not edit those parts by hand.
- The home page reaches the character pages through the character list (`characters/index.md`, 2026-11-23); `INDEX_PAGES` in `scripts/check-dist.mjs` names the index pages the check follows. New sets of pages are reached the same way, through index pages linked from the home page (owner, 2026-11-25).
- Each set of game-text pages per character has its own index page under `characters/`, one row and one link per character: `characters/archives` (with the number of entries) and `characters/voices` (with the numbers of daily and combat lines), linked from the Characters section of the home page and listed in `INDEX_PAGES`. The character list keeps Character, Class and Attribute and links no sub-pages (owner, 2026-11-26). Three links on each of its 25 rows ran past 5,000 extracted characters: the absolute addresses alone take about 4,500. A new page set per character gets an index page of its own the same way.
- Primary source: the global official site `nte.perfectworld.com`, because it is readable with a plain HTTP fetch. The Taiwan site `nte.iwplay.com.tw` is used only for Traditional Chinese names.
- Do not label the service region beside each date. The pages state once, in the home page caveat and in `llms.txt`, that dates follow the global official site in UTC+8 and that mainland China is not covered.
- Official art is used, by the owner's decision, although the publisher's Derivative Work Guidelines forbid direct copying of official materials. Credits go in the `art` frontmatter field and appear in the page notes at the end of the page, never under the picture. Remove an image when the publisher asks.
- The first screen of a page shows content. Scope, caveat, dates and sources are shown in the page notes after the body.
- No disclaimers in the visible text: do not write what a page leaves out, what the notes fail to say, or what is unverified. Leave the fact out, or state the positive fact.
- Character pages describe skills. Skill names and effects come from Prydwen and are checked against Game8; damage values, tier lists and team builds are not copied.
- Guide sites may supply a fact that the official sources lack when at least two of them agree, and the page names them in `sources`. A fact from one guide site is attributed in the sentence ("Game8 says").
- Look: pixel terminal in the official colours, cyan `#39d5fc` and pink `#fb5692`. Motion is sparing: the title is typed once, the first blocks print in once, the cursors blink. Removed as too much, by the owner's decision: the moving logo mark, the signal meter, the moving band under the header, motion and scan lines on images, the status line. Do not add them back. The site name has a mark of the look before it, drawn in CSS: two slanted cards, cyan over pink, as in `public/favicon.svg`; it does not move, the cursor after the name does (2026-11-25).
- `robots.txt` carries `ai-train=no`. Address: `https://yuanaruru.com/nte-wiki/`.
- How the site follows its sources (owner, 2026-11-22): a scheduled check that keeps one issue, as for `github-repositories`, run every day (owner, 2026-11-25; weekly before); it also compares the text of the cited notices of the current version. See "When the sources change".

## Commands

```
pnpm verify      # subset the font, build, then run the acceptance checks. Run this before you call a change done.
pnpm build       # subset the font and build
pnpm check       # acceptance checks on an existing dist/
pnpm upstream    # compare the sources with what the pages were written from (upstream/README.md)
node scripts/prose-review.mjs run      # after pnpm build: a reader of another model reads every unit of prose not yet in the ledger (PROSE_READER names it)
node scripts/prose-review.mjs status   # units of prose the ledger lacks; pnpm verify fails for each

uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented scripts/to-zh-cn.py   # write the zh-cn pages from the zh-tw pages, then run the last line below

# game text from records (after any zh-tw change, in this order; no slug = everything)
python3 pipeline/fetch_nanoka.py                                              # nanoka.cc files to pipeline/cache/
CHROMIUM=<chrome-headless-shell> node pipeline/fetch_everness.mjs $(python3 pipeline/everness_keys.py)   # Traditional Chinese
python3 pipeline/build_records.py [slug ...]                                  # records/
python3 pipeline/build_records.py --voices [slug ...]                         # records/voices/
uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented pipeline/build_records.py --monsters   # records/anomalies.json (after fetch_nanoka.py --monsters)
uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented pipeline/build_records.py --lists      # records/lists.json (after fetch_nanoka.py --lists and fetch_everness.mjs)
uv run --with opencc-python-reimplemented scripts/to-zh-cn.py
python3 pipeline/build_pages.py [slug ...]
pnpm preview     # serve dist/ at http://127.0.0.1:4399/nte-wiki/ with GitHub Pages style headers
```

## Content rules

- Pages live in `src/content/docs/` (English), `src/content/docs/zh-tw/` (Traditional Chinese), `src/content/docs/zh-cn/` (Simplified Chinese), `src/content/docs/ja/` (Japanese) and `src/content/docs/ko/` (Korean). Page addresses use the official English name in lower case: `characters/elyms`, `characters/daffodill`, `versions/1-5`.
- Do not edit `zh-cn` pages by hand. They are written from the `zh-tw` pages by `scripts/to-zh-cn.py`: it replaces Taiwan wording with the wording of the official Simplified Chinese notices, converts the characters, swaps the title with its Simplified alias and points links at `/zh-cn/`. Run it after every change to a `zh-tw` page. When a `zh-tw` page brings in a new name, check it against the notices under `nte.perfectworld.com/cn/` and add a pair to `VOC` in the script if the two differ (three did: 淘方斯记, 末班公交车之夜, 神秘纽扣). Every `zh-tw` page whose title changes in Simplified characters needs that Simplified title among its aliases.
- Write links and images root-relative and without the site's sub-path: `/characters/elyms/`, `/art/characters/elyms.webp`. In `zh-tw` pages, links start with `/zh-tw/`; image paths do not. The sub-path is added at build time (`src/lib/hast-base.mjs`).
- Required frontmatter is enforced by `src/content.config.ts`. Optional: `effectiveDate` (date of the newest official notice used), `aliases` (the subject's name in the other scripts), `art` (credit links for the images on the page). The title and aliases together are the same set of strings in every language; the check script compares them.
- `appliesTo` names the game version. `caveat` names the end of anything limited as an absolute date and time with `(UTC+8)`.
- Quote a frontmatter value that contains a colon followed by a space.
- Names: English from the English notes, Traditional Chinese from the Taiwan notices, Simplified Chinese from the `/cn/` notes. When no Taiwan text has a name, Chinese pages use the Simplified name in Traditional characters with Taiwan vocabulary (計畫, 介面); the names page leaves that Traditional cell empty. Chinese pages use the game's own Chinese names for skills, skill states and game terms (Skills, Growth, Awakenings and system tables), from the game data; the English pages keep the English names. The Skills tables in Chinese name each skill without its slot prefix (猛撞, not 普通攻擊：猛撞), except Critical Ripostes (極限反擊：惡作劇), and quote names in running text with 「」. Where Prydwen's English has no game counterpart, the Chinese uses the game's wording for the thing (Minty Whirlwind → 龍捲風). The Chinese text is converted to Traditional characters with Taiwan forms (後台, 舞台, 回復); game Simplified names in them must survive the round trip to `zh-cn` unchanged.
- Japanese and Korean pages: translated from the English page with the same facts, headings, tables and links. Names come from the Japanese and Korean game data 1.5.4 (nanoka.cc `ja/` and `ko/` files) for in-game text, and from the official Japanese (`nte.perfectworld.com/jp/`) and Korean (`/kr/`) notices for versions, events, Boards and outfits; a name with neither stays in English. Those notices write JST/KST (UTC+9); the pages keep the UTC+8 times of the English pages. Every page lists the Japanese and Korean counterparts of the notices and game data files it cites, in every language. Core terms: Arc 弧盤 / 아크, Board ボード / 보드, Esper Cycle 異能連環 / 에스퍼 사이클, Console ギア / 콘솔, Cartridge 駆動コア / 카트리지, Awakening 覚醒 / 각성; skill slots 通常攻撃, バイレールスキル, EXレール終結, サポートスキル / 일반 공격, 바이레일 스킬, 울티메이트, 서포트 스킬. Style: Japanese である調, Korean 한다체. The title and aliases of each page name the same set in all five languages.
- Attributes in Chinese: 光 Cosmos, 靈 Anima, 咒 Incantation, 魂 Psyche (Taiwan news posts); 暗 Chaos, 相 Lakshana (Traditional Chinese guide sites). Arc types in Chinese are followed by the English in parentheses, for example 固態 (Solid). The game's Chinese text names the five types 固态, 液态, 气态, 等离子 and 聚合 (Condensate); 聚合 replaced the earlier 凝聚態.
- Character pages follow one structure: portrait, Profile (with voice actors), How to obtain, Skills, Growth, Awakenings, then Signature Arc, Outfits, Story and modes where they apply. Skill multipliers comes last: one table per skill with Lv. 1–13 from the game data (`abilities[].param_rows`), labels in the page language, `*` written as `×`, and a row that is the same at every level written as a sentence under the table. Portraits go in `public/art/characters/<slug>.webp`, cropped from the official site's mobile poster (left 95, top 150, width 560, height 686 of the 750×836 image). Adler, Edgar, Haniel, Aurelia and Skia have no profile on the official site and so no portrait.
- When a version changes or a Board opens: see `upstream/README.md`.
- The home page must link every page within its first 5,000 extracted characters, or an index page that does. The body of the English home page ends about 380 characters under the limit (2026-11-26; the check counts absolute URLs); the versions table lost its column of new limited characters to make room on 2026-11-21. Shorten cells or drop duplicate links before adding rows; another page needs a change the owner decides, such as a systems index page. System pages are a plain link list there.

## When the sources change

Run `pnpm upstream`. What it watches, what each line of its report means and how to update the pages are in `upstream/README.md`; read it when an update is due.

## Skin rules

- The header links the five sections, in the sidebar's order: versions, characters, anomalies, items and achievements, systems and names (`SiteTitle.astro`, from the sidebar groups), each to its first page. The Markdown twin, `llms.txt` and `index.json` are linked from the page notes, not the header. `scripts/check-dist.mjs` fails otherwise (`checkHeaderAndOutputs`). Decided 2026-11-22: the header is for players; the format links and their key hints `[M]` `[L]` `[J]` moved out of it.
- All marks, prefixes, key hints, counters and the cursor are drawn with CSS and never typed into page text. If you add text through CSS `content`, add a pattern for it to check 5 in `scripts/check-dist.mjs`.
- Three Starlight components are overridden: `SiteTitle`, `MarkdownContent` and `ThemeSelect` (three buttons, so each theme is one click away; it keeps Starlight's storage key `starlight-theme`).
- The hidden note, the answer and the page notes are direct children of the article container, with no wrapper. A wrapper made a readability-style extractor keep the notes and drop the body. The hidden note repeats scope and caveat at the top so that they fall within the first 5,000 extracted characters while the visible notes sit after the body.
- Starlight wraps each heading in `div.sl-heading-wrapper`; sibling selectors must target the wrapper, not `h2`.
- The body font stack names Traditional Chinese fonts; `:root[lang='zh-CN']` in `nte.css` swaps in Simplified ones, so punctuation sits at the baseline on Simplified pages. `:root[lang='ja']` and `:root[lang='ko']` swap in Japanese and Korean body fonts and the pixel font cut from Fusion Pixel's `ja` and `ko` files (`public/fonts/pixel-ja.woff2`, `pixel-ko.woff2`); Korean pages break lines at spaces (`word-break: keep-all`). Every stack also names fonts of the other scripts, and the Korean stack puts a Japanese font before Malgun Gothic, whose kana are undersized; the names page mixes all five in one table.
- Table bodies carry the class `rows-scroll` (`src/lib/hast-base.mjs`), unstyled: readability-style extractors weigh it down, so a page with one long table still yields the whole article.
- The pixel font is Fusion Pixel 12px (SIL OFL; the Korean glyphs come from Galmuri, licence in `assets/fonts/LICENSES/`). The `zh_hant` file also holds every Simplified character the pages use (checked on 2026-11-18). `scripts/subset-font.mjs` cuts each source file down to the characters its pages use before every build and writes `public/fonts/pixel.woff2` (English and Chinese), `pixel-ja.woff2` and `pixel-ko.woff2`, which are not committed. Use the font at 12, 24 or 36 px only.
- Images are shown as they are, in a frame with an offset shadow: no overlay, no motion.
- Wide windows: the table of contents keeps the sidebar's width and the text column sits in the middle of the space between the two ("Wide windows" block in `nte.css`). The text column is 50rem, and 60rem from a 110rem window.
- All motion sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. Screenshots need a wait of about two seconds for the opening animation.

## Verified pitfalls (Astro 7.4, Starlight 0.43)

- Astro 7 renders Markdown with Sätteri. `markdown.remarkPlugins` fails without `@astrojs/markdown-remark`; write a Sätteri plugin (`defineHastPlugin`) and pass it through `markdown.processor: satteri({ hastPlugins })`.
- `starlight-links-validator` accepts a zh-tw link without the `/zh-tw/` prefix because the English page exists. The home page check in `check-dist.mjs` catches it.
- The official character names on `nte.perfectworld.com` are images. Take names from the patch notes.
- Official English texts write "Daffodill"; Prydwen and Game8 write "Daffodil" (Prydwen's page slug is `daffodil`).
- Vite rewrites `url('/fonts/pixel.woff2')` in `nte.css` with the site's sub-path; no manual prefix is needed.
- A float does not work inside a flex or grid container, so the page notes cannot be moved below the body with CSS `order` while the portrait floats. The notes are placed after the body in the DOM instead.
