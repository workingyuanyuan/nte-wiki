## Daily

### First Meet

I'm Shinku from CSU-2. You're Coco, the one Mint mentions a lot, right? Nice to meet you. I hope I won't give you as much trouble as the dumcat.

### Chat: Dragon Horns

They're not as useful in battle as my tail, but the horns don't bother me as much as my tail does, except when measuring my height.

### Chat: Vintage Shop

On Bagel, I noticed a few vintage shops just opened in Hethereau. Let's take a look when we can.

### Chat: Rumors

I don't have wings, I don't spit fire, and I don't grab my enemies and circle around in the sky before body-slamming them. Seriously, who made this up…?

### Sunny

The weather is so good, we might run into Mint at a square.

### Rainy

It's raining again. Tch, trouble…

### Cold

It's not like I'm cold… but let's get this over with.

### Hot

I could use an ice pop now…

### Morning Greeting

Morning. You look like you're motivated. Wanna take my patrolling shift?

### Noon Greeting

Do you want to get ramen? There's this old ramen store I used to go to when I was still in school. It tastes great.

### Evening Greeting

I hope there isn't a sudden report to turn in tonight…

### Good Night

It's getting late. You don't have to send me home, do you? See you tomorrow. Good night.

### Hobbies

I collect anything that catches my eye: from the ultra-secret editions of DSD's collab Mystery Boxes with Duke K, to misprinted issues of Weekly Fantasy—even simply cool rocks I come across by the beach. Wanna see? Maybe next time.

### Worries

My funds are running a bit low. I really don't want to touch my savings…

### Topic: CSU

The "New Containment Safety Protocol" is bothersome. There are so many rules, and whenever I'm on a mission, I'm forced to think twice before I act. Still, training myself to follow those annoying restrictions when my powers are affecting me is the reason I'm here.

### Topic: Wandering

When I don't have plans, I like walking around without a destination in mind. I'll roam into random places, and when I see things that interest me, I collect them. When I was little, I called this "treasure hunting." It's still accurate, right?

### Topic: Nacupeda

Nacupeda is an expert at keeping coins: just stash them in the pool and keep them within eyesight. No wonder that anomaly is always happy. Maybe I should do something similar?

### About Myself: Esper Ability

My former captain told me that my power is a curse. Though it makes me strong, it could also destroy the things I love. I was once depressed because I injured a friend by mistake… But now I understand: I'll embrace my power as long as I don't have to hate myself for being weak when I need to be strong.

### About Myself: Relationships

"I always go solo in the Bureau?" That dumcat just blabbers around… Well, I can't really be alone if she always drags me and Iroi around, can I?

### About Myself: Urge

My Esper Abilities affect me beyond fights. Even in everyday situations, I might suddenly do something out of line. It wouldn't be as dangerous as during a fight, but I don't know what I might end up doing—that could mean trouble.

### About Mint I

Mint "senpai" has absolutely no talent in reading the room. Even if you want to keep your distance, she still comes over to talk to you. Sigh, I still can't leave that dumcat alone.

### About Mint II

That dumcat, she never shies away from expressing her true thoughts and feelings. I don't know if I should praise her for that or just call her thick-skinned… Still, if everyone was as straightforward as Mint, things would be a lot easier.

### About Iroi I

As far as colleagues go, Iroi is pretty much perfect. I was probably paired with her because when I lose control, she can calm me down with her dreams, which was where I saw… never mind. Anyway, I don't see her as a normal person, but it doesn't affect our work.

### About Iroi II

Everyone has a story they don't want to tell. I was like that before. It's the same for Ramtenna. I'm not exactly sure what she's been through, but the least I can do is be by her side—like my teammates were by my side when we faced the Crimson Dragon. The bickering? I prefer to not bicker with her, but maybe that's what companions do.

### About CSU-2

I thought I was going to go through some troubles when I first transferred to CSU-2, but thanks to Mint, colleagues accepted me quickly. In contrast, I almost made a big blunder on my first mission. Good thing that Iroi was there to help… Ahem, I should buy my colleagues some drinks from Crazy Cat.

### About Bureau of Anomaly Control

For the record, I'm not dissing my colleagues. I just wanted to say that the BAC is made up of a bunch of weirdos, especially the CSUs and ETDs, which require fieldwork and dealing with dangerous anomalies. Now that I think about it, I was able to blend into the Bureau and learn from my former captain because there weren't many "normal people" around to begin with.

### Character Ascension I

Now I can fight stronger enemies on my own.

### Character Ascension II

This might mean trouble, but getting stronger is a good thing.

### Character Ascension III

Not enough… I need more…!

### Gift Received I

…! Don't regret giving me this.

### Gift Received II

You know what I like.

### Gift Received III

Thank you. I'll add it to my stash.

### Gift Received IV

Is there anything I can help with?
