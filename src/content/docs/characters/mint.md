## Skills

Mint is an on-field Anima attacker who chains five-hit combos, Stamina-draining Minty Whirlwind spins, dash strikes, and multi-hit AoE slashes. While on-field she gains increased DEF and interruption resistance, and her Blossom Enhancement expands the damage area of Vita Pistils.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Perfect Containment, Justice from Above, Caramel Crisp, Minty Whirlwind | Performs up to five consecutive Anima attacks, plus airborne plunge Justice from Above. Holding Basic Attack triggers Minty Whirlwind, spinning continuously to deal surrounding Anima DMG while draining Stamina. After Critical Dodge, Caramel Crisp strikes an area with Anima DMG and reduces Break. |
| Skill | Ultimate: Super Claws | Dashes forward and returns to her starting position, dealing two instances of AoE Anima DMG to enemies along the path. |
| Ultimate | Ultimate: Thunderous Whirlwind Slash | Channels wind into dual blades to deal multiple instances of AoE Anima DMG, finishing with a final strike dealing heavy AoE Anima DMG. |
| Support Skill | Ultimate: Invincible Tornado Slash | Charges and leaps into the air to deliver a penetrating attack, dealing one instance of AoE Anima DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Transform! Super Mint! | Blossom Enhancement expands the damage area of Vita Pistils on hit. |
| Passive 2 | Bingo! We're Done Here! | Increases Mint's DEF and resistance to Interruptions while she remains on the field. |
