## Daily

### First Meet

Yo, Akane here. Do I even need to introduce myself? We can do everything with our band chemistry, can't we?

### Chat: Outfits

I'm not big on fashion. I'd stick to casual wear if I didn't have to perform on stage.

### Chat: Part-Time Work

People ask all kinds of questions. Many want me to share tips for performing. Actually, there have been so many of these requests that I started asking people to buy a drink before they ask any questions. Hmm… Kiyosawa should give me a raise.

### Chat: Headset

People these days prefer wireless earphones, but I still think wired ones look and work better. Suzuha keeps making fun of me about how the headphone jack is ancient history… Is it really that bad?

### Sunny

Nice weather. I spend too much time in StarSign. I could use some sunlight.

### Rainy

Be extra careful on rainy days. Guitar strings can rust fast.

### Cold

Brrr, so cold. Let's get hot chocolate somewhere.

### Hot

It's scorching… I'm gonna stay in a room with AC all day.

### Morning Greeting

(\*Yawn\*) Morning. W-What? I didn't have a secret practice session last night…!

### Noon Greeting

I don't wanna eat at work today. Let's go grab something better. My treat.

### Evening Greeting

Ahhh, another workday has passed. Should I give myself a treat?

### Good Night

Good night. Don't use your phone too much before you sleep.

### Hobbies

My hobbies besides music? Umm… does scrolling Bagel count? Or zoning out under the stars? Kiyosawa asked me the same thing, wants me to get other hobbies. But honestly, just listening to music and playing the guitar is enough for me.

### Worries

One day it's a cool new effects pedal, the next it's a new album. You can't save Fons being in a band. I've been living paycheck to paycheck for a long time now.

### Topic: My First Guitar

My first guitar… was from my dad when I graduated from elementary school. I keep it in good shape. It still plays. Every now and then, I take it out and run through some basic scales.

### Topic: Off the Clock

Oh, are you also into efficient living? Basically, you only do things you have to, and even for those things, keep it simple. Work wears me out every day. Where would I find the energy to go out? Way better to head home early, crank up the AC, and scroll Bagel. Kids these days just don't know how good this lifestyle is.

### Topic: School Days

My student days? Let me think… I basically put everything into the band. People these days probably would have called me a slacker of a student. Though I heard back then half the girls in school were fans of mine… no idea if that's actually true.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

When I have strong feelings, white stars fly out from my fingertips the moment I pluck the strings. Pretty cool, right? As for how it works… I have no idea, and I don't care to find out. Anyway, think about it. I basically have built-in special effects when I'm totally into the performance. The effects are unreal.

### About Myself: Stars

I'm no astronomy geek but I love looking at stars. I like quietly watching them fill up the night sky. When my mind's a mess, I look up at the open sky… and knowing all that starlight traveled unimaginable distances just to reach us, that thought calms me down every time.

### About Myself: "One-Girl Band"

I'm a one-person band…? That's a stretch. You heard that from Aurelia or Suzuha, didn't you? Well, it's true that I've systematically learned all the essential band instruments. As a kid, it was because I loved how they sounded. After starting a band, it was more to understand what everyone was doing, so we could get through any rough patches together… That's how you keep a band in tune.

### About Aurelia

Aurelia's always been the thoughtful, gentle one, both in and out of the band. Without her, we'd have lost half our stuff: be it the tuner I left in the practice room or the jacket Suzuha left in the car. Back then, she handled most of the band's logistics. Now when I run into her working on Tamamochi Street, she's just as careful and just as focused. She's still the old Aurelia. Never changed a bit.

### About "THE WHOOTS!!!!"

"A noisy Owly, a loud kitty; less a band, more a circus." That was my first thought when Haniel said she wanted to start a band. Pfft… never thought we'd actually pull it off, let alone do successful live gigs. I guess that's what a band is—a place where any dream, no matter how ridiculous, comes true.
