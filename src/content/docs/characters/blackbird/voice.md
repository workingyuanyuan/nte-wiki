## Daily

### First Meet

Crystal guides me, the bird calls me. To this place I have come, our fates tangled and spun…

### Chat: Poise

"I see all fates in a mirror. I see all truths you seek…" Did I sound mysterious but authoritative? Heheh, keeping up appearances takes work. I practice quite a bit, you know.

### Chat: Collection

I love collecting all sorts of antiques. The stranger the design, the more I like them… My collection? I have more than what you usually see. I had a whole room built to store them. If you're interested, come by and take a look sometime.

### Chat: Out and About

The Witch's House is quiet and peaceful, with everything I need: snacks, comic books, and my crystal ball… Oh, you're going to pay me a visit? Ugh… so much for being a shut-in…

### Sunny

### Evening Greeting

### Good Night

The dead of night is the perfect time for a horror movie…

### Hobbies

Getting the most sale items at the lowest price is an art in itself! You have to know the timing of the promotions, how deep the discounts go, what kinds of items are on sale, and plan out your budget before you even head to the store… I-If you're interested, I could share with you my shopping tactics…

### Worries

I get dizzy once I start working. I can't keep my human form anymore…

### Topic: Anomaly

A diviner cannot see their own fate: not the future, not the past. But somehow, I can't see your past either. It seems to be hidden on the other side of the stars, and I can't even figure out its shape… Is the crystal ball deceiving me? What kind of fate do you carry? I'm curious… No, never mind…

### Topic: "Witch"

To be honest, I'm no witch at all. Apart from my anomaly powers, I don't know any kinds of spells. I've never kept a black cat, and I'm not even human in the strictest sense… Still, I've long since made peace with everyone calling me a "witch." It saves me a lot of trouble explaining. Of course, anyone would think a witch lives in the Witch's House, wouldn't they?

### Topic: Starry Sky

When the world was yet unformed, the stars had written the fate of all things. Above the veil of night, every thread of starlight we see and cannot see, every flicker, every speck of dust whispers of hidden things. Within them lie the answers to the fate of all beings.

### About Myself: Anomaly Abilities

My powers allow me to divine and cleanse Oracle Stones. I don't really know why I was given such powerful abilities… Or rather, I don't even know why I exist in this world at all. Though I've read countless people's fates, my own is hidden behind the fog that swirls within the crystal ball. Espers must pay a price for their abilities, and as an anomaly, perhaps my powers are just limited in this aspect… That said, whatever the truth of my fate turns out to be, I accept it gladly.

### About Myself: Blackbird

When I really do need to go out, or when I don't feel like working, I turn into a bird. It's easy, doesn't draw attention, and cute too, isn't it? Only you and Lavinia know this secret. Next time a bird flies past you, take a closer look and see if it's me.

### About Myself: Hat

I've given some of my powers to this hat I'm wearing. Even when I'm not around, I can sense what's happening near it. When I don't feel like seeing people, I let it act as my mouthpiece while I cozy up in the attic… I-I'm not slacking off, of course! I'm still divining for my clients, I swear…

### About Lavinia

Lavinia is a specialist sent from the Yggash Court to the Witch's House. She's my supervisor, but also my friend. Don't let her serious face fool you, she's the best cook I know. Every dish she makes turns out super delicious. Hmm, maybe she casts a spell to make the food taste better, who knows…

### About Hotori

A few times when I was low on cash, I randomly grabbed some items from my collection and tried selling them to the owner of that antique shop. I thought she'd be a shrewd businesswoman, but she turned out to be surprisingly easygoing and generous. Every time, she'd offer a price that even surprised me… Isn't she a little too easy to swindle?

### About Linko

Linkie is a great movie buddy. On her days off, she'll sometimes come over with a bunch of DVDs to watch horror movies with me all night. Every time she and Xiaozhen show up, the Witch's House gets lively again. Don't let her size fool you. I still can't figure out what wild ideas run around in that little pink head of hers. (\*Giggles\*) This kid's future is going to be full of adventures and wonders.

### About Merula

The closer I get to her, the more I feel a peace I've never known, as if… I've returned to my mother's embrace. I wasn't a good child. I wandered for centuries without ever knowing her. But it's not too late. Her blood still runs warm, her eyes see this new world through mine, and every moment Merula lost, I will give back to her.

### About Yggash Court

When I was wandering in the wilds and my wings started to grow heavy, the Yggash Court found me and formed a contract with me. From then on, I had a place I could call "home," but until the "work" is done, I can't leave their sight… Fate's gifts and its costs are always equal. That's only fair.
