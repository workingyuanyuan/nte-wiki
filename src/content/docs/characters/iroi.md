## Skills

Iroi builds Imagination through Basic Attacks, Skill, Support Skill, and allies entering Regression. Her Skill heals, buffs ATK, and sends allies into Regression, while her Ultimate drains Imagination to open a healing dream—or an offensive dream that temporarily revives one Regression ally.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Collaboration, Daydream, Lucid Dream, Wakey-Wakey, Reflexive Response | Collaboration performs up to five lamb attacks, Wakey-Wakey plunges, and Reflexive Response counters after a Critical Dodge to reduce Break; each builds Imagination. Holding Basic Attack spends Imagination for up to three Daydream hits, and holding again summons Morpheus for sustained Lucid Dream fire. |
| Skill | Future Self Continuity, Self-Identity Extension | Pressing triggers Self-Identity Extension to heal the team, grant an ATK bonus from Iroi's Base ATK, pull enemies with three lambs, and build Imagination. Holding Future Self Continuity places three allies into Regression until combat ends. Passively, any fallen ally enters Regression and builds Imagination. |
| Ultimate | 3.8-Billion-Year Mirage | Opens a dream that drains Imagination until depleted. Below the Imagination threshold, opens Dream of the Horned Gate for continuous team healing. At or above threshold, opens Dream of the Ivory Gate to deal Anima DMG, make Daydream and Lucid Dream free, and temporarily revive one Regression ally. |
| Support Skill | Bang! | Strikes forward with Morpheus to deal one instance of Anima DMG, heal all team members, and build Imagination. |
| Passive 1 | Mirror Image | Provides a Blossom Enhancement that spawns one Vita Bud Copy once a Vita Bud persists, firing Copied Vita Pistils at nearby targets. |
| Passive 2 | Sympathetic Nerves | Grants all team members additional DEF Ignore when performing healing. |
