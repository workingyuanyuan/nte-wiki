## Profile

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class | S-Class, the player character |
| Attribute | Cosmos |
| Arc type | Solid |
| Joined | Version 1.0 |

Zero is the player character, the Appraiser. The official profile describes an Esper who emerged from nothingness with an uncanny sense for the "Nexus" inside every anomaly, sent by the Bureau to work at the Eibon antique shop without an anomaly hunter licence. Zero can be played as male or female.

## How to obtain

Zero is the character every player starts with.

## Skills

Zero deals Cosmos DMG with Appraisal combos, fires an Esper Cannon via Appraise and Engrave that adds a hit against lower-level targets, and bursts with Divide by Zero. Appraiser restores ally HP via Charge Enhancement whenever the active character gains Ultimate Energy.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Appraisal, Appraisal - Severance, Appraisal - Break, Critical Riposte: Flash of Inspiration | Appraisal - Severance performs up to five consecutive Cosmos attacks. Appraisal - Break plunges from mid-air for area Cosmos DMG scaling with fall height. Critical Riposte: Flash of Inspiration counters after a Critical Dodge for area Cosmos DMG and reduces Break. |
| Skill | Appraise and Engrave | Performs three consecutive weapon strikes, leaps into the air to charge, and fires an Esper Cannon dealing area Cosmos DMG, plus an extra Cosmos hit to the first target below Zero's level. |
| Ultimate | Divide by Zero | Sweeps away unseen lines in a dance of blades, dealing multiple instances of Cosmos DMG to an area. |
| Support Skill | Rift Blossom | Slashes through space and detonates a rift, dealing two instances of Cosmos DMG to an area. |
| Passive 1 | Appraiser | Applies Charge Enhancement, restoring HP based on Zero's Base ATK whenever the active character gains Ultimate Energy. |
| Passive 2 | Anomaly Perception | Increases the damage dealt by Ultimate: Divide by Zero. |
