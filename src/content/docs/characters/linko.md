| Fact | Value |

The official profile says Linko was recruited into ETD-6 as a special exception. She uses telepathy to sense the "frequencies" of humans and anomalies, and leaves the fighting to her adopted anomaly companion, Xiaozhen.

## Skills

Linko builds Modulation through Basic Attacks and applies Hit 'Em Here! marks off-field to summon allies or Xiaozhen for attribute-matched Synced Strikes. Her Ultimate enters Overdrive to deploy a Resonance Field that chains Synced Strikes and turns ally Support Skills into Follow-up Attacks.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Poltergeist CQC, Spectral Smack, Spectral Entrance, Spectral Rush, Ghosthand Counter | Performs up to five Anima hits building Modulation points to empower Full-Frequency Pulse's Synced Strike, plus held Spectral Smack, airborne plunge Spectral Entrance, and Critical Dodge counter Ghosthand Counter. Off-field, Spectral Rush periodically sends Xiaozhen to ambush and apply a Hit 'Em Here! mark. |
| Skill | Full-Frequency Pulse | Deals up to four Anima hits and opens a selection wheel to summon an ally or Xiaozhen for a Synced Strike matching their attribute. Consumes all Modulation for extra Synced Strike DMG Bonus, or buffs Xiaozhen further for each missing or unable-to-act ally. |
| Ultimate | Oversync | Enters Overdrive, deploying a Resonance Field and Syncing with up to three allies so Redirect Skill triggers up to three Synced Strikes with bonus Anima AoE hits. Ends with an Anima burst scaling with target debuffs; afterward, ally Support Skills inside count as Follow-up Synced Strikes. |
| Support Skill | Spectral Cross | Strikes with Xiaozhen for AoE Anima DMG. Also summons an ally for a Synced Strike when breaking a Hit 'Em Here! mark or casting Full-Frequency Pulse or Oversync, scaling Synced Strike DMG with Spectral Cross's level. |
| Passive 1 | Weakness Radar | Hexed Enhancement raises the base DMG of additional Follow-up Attacks from Hexed while Linko is on the team, and causes targets affected by Hexed to take increased Follow-up Attack DMG. |
| Passive 2 | Precision Tuning | Reduces a target's Esper Ability Resistance matching the attribute of the character triggering a Synced Strike. Resistance Reductions of different attributes coexist without stacking per attribute, refreshing when retriggered. |
