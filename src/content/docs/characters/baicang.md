## Profile

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class | S-Class, standard |
| Attribute | Incantation |
| Arc type | Condensate |
| Joined | Version 1.0 |

The official profile introduces Baicang as the captain of ETD-4 at the Bureau of Anomaly Control: carefree, irreverent, blunt to a fault and a master of dodging work.

## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair, according to Game8 and Prydwen |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan. How Boards work: [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Skills

Baicang generates Power Words—Bless, Silence, and Objurgate—at a current HP cost using Basic Attacks, dodge combos, and Generous Guidance variants, then triggers their bonus damage, healing, and low-HP execution effects inside his Judgment of Autumn domain.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Walk the Talk, Formless Words, Unrestrained Words, In the Wind, Critical Riposte: Truth Exposed, Shadow Follows | Walk the Talk performs up to five Incantation attacks, with Shadow Follows generating a Power Word Bless every three hits up to three words at a current HP cost. Formless Words and Unrestrained Words fire three detonating commands. In the Wind plunges; Critical Riposte: Truth Exposed generates Bless. |
| Skill | Generous Guidance, Heart of Heaven and Earth, Silenced Thought, Such Crime | Heart of Heaven and Earth attacks at the target location with ley-line text. Pressing Redirect Skill after a dodge casts Silenced Thought for single-target Incantation DMG and Power Word Silence; casting after Formless Words or Unrestrained Words triggers Such Crime for area DMG and Power Word Objurgate. |
| Ultimate | Judgment of Autumn | Releases a domain and enters Power Word state, dealing Incantation DMG over time and executing targets below low HP thresholds. Grants temporary fatal-hit immunity that ends the domain and spreads recorded damage. Triggers extra single-target damage for Silence, area damage for Objurgate, and healing plus damage for Bless. |
| Support Skill | Break Time Over | Charges briefly and writes incantation commands to deal one instance of area Incantation DMG, generating one Power Word Silence on the target on hit. |
| Passive 1 | Moderate Mischief | Scorch Enhancement: Inflicts another instance of Scorch with the same effect when a Power Word is generated on a Scorch-affected target. |
| Passive 2 | Moderate Working | Increases Baicang's ATK and triggers coordinated effects with ETD-4 teammates: attacking and generating Power Word Silence when switching in during Skia's stealth, attacking and healing Fadia when her HP drops low, or triggering a super jump when switching in during Lacrimosa's bat form. |
