## Daily

### First Meet

Hello. I'm Hathor, elite courier of Sterry Express. As per request, please confirm the delivery and sign your name on the Proof of Receipt. The commission fee will be settled once the receipt is confirmed.

### Chat: Brake Pads

Ceramic brake pads maintain performance under high heat, ideal for rapid-response emergencies. Semi-metal pads cool faster and are cheaper, and are the best daily driver. Sintered metal pads handle long, high-intensity braking, perfect for mountain routes. Using the right type in the right scenario minimizes delivery delays and ensures tasks are completed on time.

### Chat: Elite Courier

Primarily responsible for Class III anomaly deliveries, intel reconnaissance for high-risk jobs, and emergency response. Explosions, car chases, aerial maneuvers…? That's just Haniel exaggerating. She enjoys those movie plots. The reality is, it's just racing against chaos.

### Chat: Cheek Pouches

Some anomaly containers require strict storage conditions—even slight temperature shifts can change their behavior. Keeping them in a Cheek Pouch stabilizes them. You could think of it like a picky pet pouch—moody and fussy, but it never fails a job.

### Worries

Ever since my mentor told me to learn how younger generations live, Haniel's been sharing all kinds of content. Some of them are interesting, but some ideas… "Why be serious when you can be crazy?" or "Stand back! My power is about to go berserk!"… I don't get it. Having power you can't control is not a good thing.

### Topic: Delivery Routes

Priority is based on urgency and risk level, then route overlap. Weather, road conditions, and high level anomaly activity zones are all factored in before finalizing a route.

### Topic: Road Cycling

Outside delivery work, I sometimes ride mountain roads with no fixed plan… Just to wander. If you want scenic routes, I can send you a guide later… I'll send them in a file on Bagel.

### Topic: Food

I don't have strong preferences for food. To me, food's just fuel. But if I must choose… sandwiches. Plenty of calories, stable structure, perfect for work.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

Sometimes my ability spirals out of control and I lose consciousness. I'm considered a threat to city stability and should be restrained. My mentor was the only one who could stabilize me back then. She vouched for me and kept me close. After a lot of training, my control has improved. But if I ever lose it during a mission, and neither my mentor nor Haniel is nearby… end me. Don't hesitate.

### About Myself: Wings

After awakening my ability, it appeared on my back and I can feel it like a limb. Why only one wing…? It's not broken. Just undeveloped. Doctors said that if I learn to control it, it might just become whole at last.

### About Myself: Thorns

Based on mentor's records, each time my power flares, thorns begin to spread. I've compressed them to the smallest stable range possible—it's still not fully under control.

### About Jenson

Most of the time he just isn't there for the business. If you can't reach him, he's probably chatting in some bar.

### About Jiuyuan

Since founding Sterry, mentor's workload grew heavier. Her lights are often on deep into the night. I want to get stronger. Strong enough to lighten her burden.

### About Haniel

We met by chance. Back then, I couldn't control my powers. Haniel helped stabilize me. Later, she joined Sterry and taught me a lot about the modern youth life… Rather than teacher and student, she prefers to call us friends. Last time we met? Probably at the anime convention… She was… energetic.

### About Illica

Whatever he's doing, he's always fired up, spouting weird lines. If you know what he's been watching lately, you can kind of make sense of what he says.

### About Sterry Express

Early on, my only contact was with my mentor. She and Jenson founded Sterry. In the early days, even small delivery volumes were overwhelming. After Haniel and Illica joined, things ran smoother, and it all felt a little more… alive.
