### Good Night

In my village, they always say, "A night of peaceful, dreamless sleep means a day of good fortune ahead." Goodnight, I hope tomorrow is your lucky day.

### Hobbies

I like Fons… hmm, I do. Fons are essential, you know? Food, medicine, clothes… none of that appears out of thin air. They all cost money. But sometimes, I wonder if Fons are really that important… I mean, once I save up enough to move back home, maybe they won't matter so much. Still, that's an enormous amount of Fons. I'd probably have to work at Pink Paws Bank until I retire.

### Worries

Problems, huh… When I first started at Pink Paws Bank, I couldn't do anything right… I'm not being modest, I mean it. I used to stand in that gorgeous lobby in my new uniform, but I couldn't speak to anyone. I was scared of screwing things up, I even considered quitting. But then…  Klingsna stepped in. She's… incredible, like the sun breaking through a storm. If only I could shine half as bright as her… I know I'm nowhere near her level, but honestly, I'm happy with my life as it is.

### Topic: Anomalies

Hethereau… is a great city. Ordinary people, anomalies, and Espers all live together as equals. It's safe and peaceful. Things aren't like this where I come from. Most anomalies are dangerous and you have to avoid them. Though… some of them can be used as food… Oh, sorry, that's probably not very interesting. But if you're curious… we can talk about it sometime later.

### Topic: Movies

When I'm off work, I watch movies at home. Most movies are interesting, because they take me to places I've never been to. I… I haven't traveled a lot, so every time I watch a movie, I can't help but think I'd love to visit those places if I could. One documentary even featured my hometown… Umm, I probably spoke too much. Just… forget what I said. Maybe… we can watch a movie together? If it's with you, even a horror movie would… would be okay!

### Topic: Convenience Store

If you visit 2-Fours at night, they sometimes sell rice balls at a discounted price. They taste just as good as the ones sold in the morning, but they're way cheaper. Perfect for a late-night snack. The only downside is you have to eat them the same day, since they expire soon. Umm… I-I'm good at making rice balls too. Would you… ever want to try some?

### About Myself: Hoarding

When I find things I like, I end up… collecting a lot of them. Like, a lot. But I promise, I… I keep everything neat and organized. It's just… a habit. It's like, I feel more secure when I have a huge pile of stuff all together.

### About Myself: Esper Ability

E-Esper abilities… They're… r-really handy. Super con-convenient, ex-extremely useful. I-I'm an Esper, so… so thanks to… to these powers, I was able to move to Hethereau. I was also able to fi-find work easily, because of my abilities. Being an Esper here is… is really great. I like it here.

### About Myself: Bandages

Does it… look bad? I used to be… bad at everything, always getting myself hurt… B-but I'm better now! I don't get hurt as much anymore. It's just… my old scars aren't pretty, so I covered them. A coworker recommended these bandages. They're soft and breathable, so they don't feel icky. Please don't worry about me. The wounds have all healed up. They don't hurt anymore.

### About Dvořák

The Dvořák family, they're rather… unusual, I guess? No, no, I swear I don't mean that in a bad way! When I first got to Hethereau, I was taken in by their shelter. Thanks to their foundation, I was able to get an education. After that, I started working at Pink Paws Bank. Pretty much everyone in Hethereau—locals or outsiders alike—has benefited from them at some point. It's like the Dvořáks are always… giving money away? They don't even ask for anything in return. Maybe that's what makes them good people.
