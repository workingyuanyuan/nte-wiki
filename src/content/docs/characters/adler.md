## Skills

Adler builds Karma stacks through Basic Attacks and consumes them with his Skill, Evil's Bane, to deal area Incantation DMG over time and grant a Blessing shield, while his Ultimate deals multi-hit Incantation DMG and his Temperance passive debuffs Scorch-afflicted enemies.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Deliverance, Liberation, Ignorance, Critical Riposte: Revelation, Karma | Performs up to five consecutive Incantation attacks with Deliverance, aims a single-target shot with Liberation, or plunges with Ignorance. Casting Deliverance after a Critical Dodge triggers Critical Riposte: Revelation to reduce Break. Hits from Deliverance, Ignorance, and Liberation grant Karma up to 20 stacks. |
| Skill | Evil's Bane | Summons Sunya phantoms to deal area Incantation DMG, inflict Incantation DMG over time, and grant active characters a shield with Blessing. Consumes all stacks of Karma on cast, increasing Skill DMG per stack consumed. |
| Ultimate | Tranquility | Summons five Sunyas—Rupa, Vedana, Sanna, Vinnana, and Sankhara—to strike all enemies for five instances of Incantation DMG each. When only a single enemy remains on the field, the Sunyas strike a second time for ten total instances of Incantation DMG. |
| Support Skill | Pristine Reflection | Aims and fires one Compressed Round at the enemy, dealing one instance of area Incantation DMG on hit. |
| Passive 1 | Temperance | Scorch Enhancement: Whenever Scorch is inflicted on a target, applies one of three random debuffs that reduce ATK, reduce Esper RES, or increase Break Efficiency. The same debuff effect cannot stack. |
| Passive 2 | Righteous Heart | Increases Adler's DEF. |

## Awakenings

Each duplicate unlocks one Awakening of the player's choice; Resonances unlock at 3 and 6 Awakenings.

| Awakening | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Form | Grants Adler 20 stacks of Karma when casting Tranquility. |
| Sensation | Each stack of Karma grants Adler 3% DMG Bonus. |
| Perception | Increases the Shield of Blessing by 20%. |
| Formation | Absorbs a portion of overflow damage based on 50% of Adler's DEF when Blessing breaks. |
| Discernment | Grants Adler 20 Ultimate Energy when casting Tranquility. |
| All Forms Are Void | Extends the duration of Blessing to 12s. |
| Resonance 3: Cleanse | Increases the skill level of Deliverance, Evil's Bane, and Tranquility by 1. |
| Resonance 6: Enlightened Guardian | Increases team's Incantation DMG by 10% while protected by Blessing. |
