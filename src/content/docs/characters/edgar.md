## Skills

Edgar heals allies through his channeled Skill and Ultimate domain, earns Key of Truth stacks from Skills and Support Skills to extend his Ultimate duration, and restores Ultimate Energy via Charge Enhancement.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Combat Practice, Calculated Landing, Critical Riposte: Crisis is Opportunity | Combat Practice performs up to five consecutive Cosmos DMG attacks. Calculated Landing plunges while airborne for height-scaled area Cosmos DMG. Using Combat Practice after a Critical Dodge triggers Critical Riposte: Crisis is Opportunity, charging forward to unleash a Flow attack that deals area Cosmos DMG and reduces Break. |
| Skill | Wild Current | Hold to channel Current at the target, dealing Cosmos DMG and continuously healing the teammate with the lowest HP. Its cooldown increases based on how long it is channeled. |
| Ultimate | Finnegan's Wake | Unleashes a labyrinthine domain that deals one instance of AoE Cosmos DMG and heals the active character over its duration. |
| Support Skill | Weight of Knowledge | Charges toward the target and swings his bag, dealing one instance of area Cosmos DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Gentle Edge | Charge Enhancement instantly grants Ultimate Energy when a character triggers Charge with a Support Skill, then temporarily disables Ultimate Energy gain from Vita Pistils hitting Slowed targets. |
| Passive 2 | Unchanging Warmth | Casting Redirect Skill Wild Current or Support Skill Weight of Knowledge grants a Key of Truth, up to three keys; each key extends the duration of his Ultimate (referred to as Finnegan's Vigil). |
