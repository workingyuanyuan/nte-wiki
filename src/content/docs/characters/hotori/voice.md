## Daily

### First Meet

A new face… A guest…? …Wait… isn't that my Precious?! Ugh… I'm so dizzy I can't even tell… I need a hangover cure…

### Chat: Reputation

When you're out on a job, make sure to protect our shop's rep. Cough… And for the record, I couldn't care less about the low ratings for Eibon on Hyelp. Numbers on a screen, nothing more! Real gratitude from real customers is what matters!

### Chat: Fons

Fons, Fons, Fons… Who came up with that name? The more I say it, the better it sounds… "Fons"… what a lovely word.

### Chat: Treasure Hunt

Look after the shop for me, I'm heading to the flea market at Bridge Crossings… What for? Heh, treasure hunting, of course.

### Sunny

I'm not like those kids with endless energy to burn, running around every day. I'll wait for a nice, shady day before I go out again.

### Rainy

Listening to this rain outside… I just had a great idea… Our yard feels a bit empty, doesn't it? What about a koi pond?

### Cold

In this city, even the coldest season doesn't feel too bad… and snow's rare. Honestly, I kinda miss home sometimes.

### Hot

Ugh, it's boiling out here… Let me know before you grab the chilled sake, okay? Otherwise it'll warm up in no time.

### Morning Greeting

Who is it?! That brat or Taygedo?! Clattering around downstairs at night… They're in for a lesson today.

### Noon Greeting

Ugh… this is the drowsiest time of day. I'm gonna rest somewhere cool.

### Evening Greeting

Today's revenue is… how is it in the red again?

### Good Night

Great drink… Hmph, "The glass is already empty"? Yeah, I noticed. Just look at this moonlight. It's free. And hey, it offers unlimited refill, right? Fill it up again, to the brim.

### Hobbies

These days, what hobby doesn't cost money? Honestly, chatting with Daff is more fun. Sip a little, tease her a bit, enjoy the reaction… now that's good entertainment.

### Worries

Why's the utility bill this high… Edgar, use that clever little brain of yours and double-check this number… What!? Commercial rates?! Tch… all these years and I'm only finding this out now…

### Topic: Anomaly - "Melancholic Dandy"

If you're bored, go chat with that painting. Try to be natural and say something nice. I'm tired of hearing it moan about how its face is cracking. Last time I passed by, it even asked for skincare cream to dab between the brushstrokes! Absurd!

### Topic: Transaction Guidelines

There are a few lines in Eibon's terms of trading service that aren't exactly the best things to let customers know… Yeah, I know. When I first wrote them, I just jotted down what I felt… Still, trade's all about honesty, right? If anything sounds off, just cross it out before showing the client. No need to reprint. Printing shops these days are daylight robbery… Shops honest as Eibon aren't very likely to survive in this evil world…

### Topic: Commission Agreement

Hey, Precious, come help me out. I'm thinking of adding a line to our commission form… something like, "A 50% service fee applies if you request anomaly appraisal by our in-house Appraiser." What do you think?… What's with that head shake? Fine—80%! Deal!

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

"Time" isn't just a path. It's also… a point of view.

### About Myself: Judgment

Through the lens of "Time," I've appraised countless rare artifacts. You gotta have that kind of eye to run an antique shop… What? My judgments have been off lately? Hmm… Maybe it's 'cause Precious here is just too dependable and I've been letting my guard down too much…

### About Myself: Proprietress

What do you mean I'm a hands-off boss? Which brat told you that? I've always handled Eibon's inventory. And besides, Adler's managing the operations. My job is to make decisions for the business! That's where leadership is.

### About Adler

Look, look, look—see those fine lines around Adler's eyes? Popped up like mushrooms after rain since we opened. The little troublemakers have no idea how much we adults stress over them.

### About Daffodill

Daff doesn't drink much on her own, but if you say cheers and invite her to a toast, she'll down one after another. I told her, after toasting, you'll have to drink at least half a glass—it's basic etiquette. Heh heh… and then… who knows what fun you'll see? Her tolerance isn't what she thinks it is.

### About Sakiri

Sakiri's a clever one. She's got potential to be a great little shopkeeper. But this habit of undercutting me? I'll need to straighten that out properly someday…

### About Taygedo

When I first found Taygedo, I was thrilled. Thought I could save on buying a TV. But now the kids hog it every day, watching anime and what not… Sigh. One day, when I strike it rich, I'm getting a screen as big as a wall…

### About Nanally

Nanally! What did you break this time?! Why'd the compensation notice show up at our door without warning?! …Where'd that little rascal go… Tch, Adler! Listen, one month—argh, maybe one week! No eggrolls for her. Make her remember this time… and don't sneak her any!

### About Edgar

Edgar's such a quiet, well-behaved kid. Loves books, doesn't ask for much. How did his parents raise him so well? I need to take notes…

### About Alphard

Keep an eye on our dear Director. Don't let her catch you slipping. I don't want to get dragged into another "parent-teacher meeting." Every time she pulls that cold face, even the premium guest couch starts feeling stiff… Honestly, it's probably less comfy than the ratty old one at home.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

I should've knew this location was no good. Cheap rent led me into a dead end—business is a bust! So why still choose to open Eibon here? Heh, because there's no such thing as "should've known." It's fate.

### Character Ascension I

I'd say… the universe might just start working in this place's favor.

### Character Ascension II

Gotta spend when it counts. Adler, bring me that bottle I've been saving!

### Character Ascension III

"Now" might just be the most memorable moment of all… who knows?

### Gift Received I

Ooh! Where did you get this? You have great taste! I like it!

### Gift Received II

This is the real deal! We got another precious one now!

### Gift Received III

Precious, you're trying really hard… Appreciate it!

### Gift Received IV

Well, we're family, right? Don't be a stranger!
