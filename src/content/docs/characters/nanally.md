## Skills

Nanally chains uninterrupted five-hit combos into Heavy Hitter! strikes while empowering herself with Ichi-daime's Authority to trigger follow-up attacks on team Esper Cycle DMG. Her Ultimate summons Underboss to pull enemies and coordinate follow-up strikes, while Blossom Enhancement accelerates Vita Bud fire.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Colucci Secret Skill, Heavy Hitter!, Colucci Secret Gundo, Grand Entrance!, Can't Touch This! | Up to five Anima claw hits that dodging does not interrupt, plus Aim-mode Colucci Secret Gundo and plunge Grand Entrance!. Holding Basic Attack chains three Heavy Hitter! strikes after specific hits or Critical Riposte. After Critical Dodge, Can't Touch This! kicks an area and reduces Break. |
| Skill | Colucci Howling Technique | Deals five instances of Anima DMG to nearby enemies and grants the Ichi-daime's Authority state. Ends when Nanally switches out, upon recasting, or after its duration expires. |
| Ultimate | Colucci Ultimate Technique | Deals seven immediate Anima hits to surrounding enemies and summons Underboss. While Underboss is active, Nanally's attacks slightly pull nearby enemies and Underboss coordinates with every attack to launch strikes that count as follow-up attack damage. |
| Support Skill | Justice from Above | Charges with a spinning motion and delivers a kick, dealing one instance of AoE Anima DMG. |
| Passive 1 | More Than Passionate | Blossom Enhancement causes each Vita Bud to fire ten Vita Pistils with a reduced firing interval. |
| Passive 2 | Fair Duel | While in the Ichi-daime's Authority state, triggers an Anima follow-up attack scaling with Basic Attack level on a single enemy whenever any teammate deals Esper Cycle DMG. |
