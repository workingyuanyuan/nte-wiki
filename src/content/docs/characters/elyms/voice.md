## Daily

### First Meet

Hey, hey, it's-a me! Long time no see, huh? How've you been…? Me? I'm currently the lieutenant of ETD Squad 6 at the BAC. Haha, betcha didn't see that coming! My name…? What, don't tell me you can't remember my name!

### Chat: You Around?

A lot of people can't stand getting a "u there?" before the other person even says what they want. And honestly, whether you're there or not depends on what others are about to say. If it's something you'd rather avoid, part of you wishes you could just not be there. Funny how people's minds work, right?

### Chat: Start a Conversation

When it comes to kicking off a chat, I figure the safest bet is to cut to the chase and say whatever you want, I guess? Take me, for example, hah, I'm here to check in on you! Anything happened lately you feel like sharing with a friend? Happy things, worrying things, anything goes!

### Chat: Continue Chatting

Some people are so scared of awkward silence that they won't even start a conversation. It's a bit backwards, but it's understandable. When that happens, you just have to read the room to figure out where to take things. Your safest pivot is something like the weather or current events. Though those can backfire too… Sometimes you're just not lucky…

### Sunny

With great weather like this, you gotta head out and pack a full schedule, right?

### Rainy

Aren't raindrops… like phone cables between the sky and the land?

### Cold

Here, hold this. Just grabbed it from the hot drinks section at the store. It's perfect for warming hands.

### Hot

I grabbed some ice cream on my way here. Here! Isn't this your favorite flavor?

### Morning Greeting

"Morning, mornin', rise and shine, wakey wakey, wake up"… There's so much nuance between them. How fun…

### Noon Greeting

Dreams from siestas tend to be nicer than dreams at night. Is it because they're "daydreams"?

### Evening Greeting

The setting sun… always makes me feel it's time to say goodbye to a friend. The whole vibe is bittersweet.

### Good Night

Rest is necessary for the human body. But once you fall asleep, your mind lets its guard down and whatever's buried in your subconscious emerges to the surface, so you can't really just relax. It's tough.

### Hobbies

Spending hours with friends on the phone, sending emails back and forth, sending voice messages, video calls, or just tap-tap-typing away… These activities all have unique flavors, and I love every one of these ways to keep in touch! Besides that, I've actually got a whole bunch of random hobbies… Umm… oh, and I love video games!

### Worries

If something bothers me, I'd say it's work. Not the job itself, but work relationships… Don't worry about me, though. It's serious, don't get me wrong, it's just that… there's probably no way to fix it, so there's no point worrying at all.

### Topic: Contact Name

It's super easy to change names. If you ask me, I could easily give you a new one. That's why I say it's about the same as setting a nickname for a contact on social media. Besides, a contact's nickname can sometimes matter more than their real name. Would you prefer I give you one of those special nicknames, like "Coco" or "Precious"?

### Topic: Casework

I could take a page from Exe's playbook and work the dark side of the net where no one notices… But tapping the open social web is a different story. I'm the only one who can easily chat with people for intel that pushes investigations forward. Having friends in all walks of life makes things easier. And if I need a friend, I'll just make one on the spot, hah. Of course, people aren't always reliable. That's where Linko comes in. She picks up important emotional cues. Once we lock onto a target, Chaos enters the picture. He usually carries out orders without further issues. If things go sideways, I port in to help… Oh, assuming he remembers to call.

### Topic: Contact

All forms of connection come to an end. Day after day, people take such connections for granted and lose sight of the fact that they can end at all. Picture every relationship as a data cable around your finger. If you could clearly watch each one go slack, coil, spring back, pull taut, fray, split open, right up until it snaps… wouldn't people be more willing to send that extra message and make that extra call to pour their heart out to people they care about?

### About Myself: Work

For Exe, I check his write-ups and fix all the parts that don't read like human speech. Sometimes I log into his account to be his mouthpiece, or smooth over the department relationships he single-handedly, unintentionally, managed to sour. For Chaos, I sync mission intel during ops. I also stand ready to run keyword analysis whenever his messages mention some modern thing he obviously can't name. For Linko, I stay on the line so I can back her up the second she's isolated. I make surprise calls to make sure she sleeps early, wakes early, heads home, and pulls no all-nighters. And I check in with her parents about her work, then join them in praising their daughter…

### About Myself: Communications

Our squad's private comms isn't set up by Chuppa, but by my own special build. I base the system on "humans with social ties." The upside is it can transmit more than voice data and generate objects straight to the scene, including my avatars or tools exported by Exe. The downside is it barely works out in the uninhabited zones far from the city, or inside anomalous realms cut off from the network. Oh, if you absolutely have to reach me somewhere like that, there's a more bothersome way: you'd have to sink yourself into your subconscious for a bit…

### About Myself: Existence

Do I exist? You see the character model, interfaces, text, you hear this voice clip, and you know that it's all just ones and zeros—illusions put together from numbers and signals. So I guess that for you, the moment all this stops meaning anything, I might as well have never existed! As for meaning, that has to come from "why" you exist—the question of "why (Y)." Funny thing, from the very moment I came into this world, I already had my "Y." So here's the response to your question: "Yes. To me, I exist."

### About Exe

Let me think… "1. With Exe, work is divided efficiently, yielding outstanding results; 2. Our daily communication is sincere and direct, with both parties comfortable raising whatever needs to be said; 3. Each party has developed a thorough understanding of the other's working style, habits, and pain points; Additional note: both parties confirm there has been no internal conflict over work matters to date, and none is anticipated going forward." …Hmm, good, I'll go with that. All truth, no lies.

### About Chaos

Picking a ringtone for Chaos is a bit of a headache. To keep comms reaching the frontline, I need him to pick up the second he hears the ring. If the tone's too mellow, he'll tune it out when he's lost in a fight or hot on a target's trail. But if it's too loud, he'll complain to me… Ah! I got it! The game music that plays on the level-clear results screen in "Tetrominoes." That one's upbeat, right?

### About Linko

Linko frets over discordant frequencies. She always tries so hard to keep us from fighting. She still doesn't get the reason, but there's a good chance she'll come close to our secrets down the line… What am I gonna do when that happens? Am I really gonna have to say something like, "So, are you siding with Exe or with me?" Ugh… what kind of face would she make… And honestly, being the only one stewing over this and drawing up contingency plans annoys the heck out of me.

### About Bureau of Anomaly Control

The Bureau's intention in making a pact with me was, first and foremost, to constrain me, to drastically slow down my expansion. Putting my abilities to use was secondary. I'm well aware of that. Believe me. That said, I was happy to help from the start. Think about it, keeping the society I depend on as stable as possible is what lets everyone feel safe enough to make wonderful memories with their friends, right?

### About ETD-6

The Bureau considers a four-person squad the optimal comp for ETD units. Once a squad hits that number, they stop considering adding anyone else. Hmm, you think our team is a bit crowded? And Nitsa's lined up to join as a reserve…? That's because the headcount doesn't apply to anomaly partners. See, Xiaozhen also isn't counted. Our squad still has one spot.

### About ETD-4

Whenever their squad heads out to the field, I get this feeling, "What a tight-knit bunch," and I can't help but smile. In places like school or work, where you don't get to choose the people around you, having partners you click with, who share some common ground, or simply just get you, is something you can only wish for.
