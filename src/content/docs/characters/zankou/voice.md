## Daily

### First Meet

I'm a skeleton in your closet, an illusion in your reflections, a nightmare in your dreams… Embrace me, and sink.

### Chat: Lone Stride

At this point, I don't care where I am anymore…

### Chat: Infamy

When you run into big shots, try telling them about the "Red Nightshade." You might see some faces twitch.

### Chat: Sightings

I've seen my fair share of dignified, well-mannered people at Mother's auctions. I glimpsed into their hidden desires. They covet wealth, long for immortality, hunger for power, or want to witness a miracle… Those are all but hollow delusions and tired clichés. But when they finally run out of road, with the dead end of having them by the throat, that raw despair… now that, that has some flavor to it.

### Sunny

Flowers will grow well in this weather.

### Rainy

No rain can ever wash away old blood stains from years ago.

### Cold

Here. Gaze into my eyes. You won't feel cold anymore…

### Hot

Such heat…

### Morning Greeting

Good morning, Appraiser… What? Still getting used to this?

### Noon Greeting

How cozy… Take a nap if you want to. I won't bother you.

### Evening Greeting

It's getting late… You're having a good time.

### Good Night

People only reveal their true selves late at night… Appraiser, want to have a heartfelt conversation?

### Hobbies

Sure. Let me indulge your curiosity. My hobbies are… tea, wine, and floristry. I dabble. But if I had to pick a favorite, it would be hunting. I love toying with my prey, watching them struggle in their illusions, running and hiding in corners they deem safe, until they realize they have nowhere to go and lose their last shred of will to survive… Care to give it a try? Hunt with me… or perhaps, be my prey?

### Worries

…Those are things of the past. They don't matter anymore.

### Topic: Family

When Mother was in a bad mood, she loved to use "training" as an excuse to punish us physically. After she punished us, she'd start crying those fake tears while saying things like "Mother forgives you." That was her way of raising us. But for the four of us… Daffodill was made of iron. The whip could come down on her and she wouldn't even blink. I could see straight through Mother's intentions, so of course I wasn't affected. As for Poinsette… her fake tears were more convincing than Inanna's. Watching the two of them put on their mother-and-daughter act was one of my favorite ways to pass the time.

### Topic: An Old Story

The world isn't all sunshine and rainbows. In its dark corners, plenty make their living off the edge of a blade, preying on the weak and endlessly fighting each other for treasure, anomalies, or some Esper. As for Inanna… before arrogance clouded her judgment, she was shrewd, calculating, and ambitious. She accrued all the wealth of infamy for the Scarlet Letter. Daffodill and I were responsible for dealing with her rivals… who, of course, were mostly the same type of person she was.

### Topic: Weapons

This blade is called Fatebinder. It's another one of Mother's collections. It creates those living, cloth-like drapes. Back then, I picked this blade because it caught my eye out of a pile of dull weapons. At that time, Daffodill loved maintaining weapons. The first time she saw mine, she thought the blade was stained with blood and offered to teach me how to polish and care for it… Shame, the blade was bloodred to begin with. No amount of scrubbing could ever change that.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

This eye is called "The Eye of Delusion." It can detect emotions, confuse minds, and erode one's reason, making them impulsive, fragile, and fall in a whirlpool of feelings. With a little preparation, it can also create illusions and even trap people inside. As for the price… every time I use my Esper Ability, the eye grows restless, with a hunger to feed on negative emotions from others. Oh, and if you meet my gaze for too long, your mind might get worn down, until you end up a plaything I can command however I please… Alright, don't turn away. That's just a joke.

### About Myself: Color Vision

In my earliest memories, the world only ever had two colors: black and white. When my powers awoke, a third color filled the void. It hid beneath gray skins. It was sometimes blinding, sometimes murky, but always vivid, always warm… You're frowning again. Do you think I'll ever be able to see the color on that flawless face of yours, Appraiser…?

### About Myself: Observation

Sometimes I disguise myself as someone else, then I walk through the crowds to observe this city… It's so accepting, so unfocused, that almost everyone who settles here convinces themselves they've found where they belong. Some even end up throwing away their own truth, forgetting it, just to protect that sense of belonging… Appraiser, are you one of them?

### About Daffodill

She has a pure soul, like a steady white flame that never burns out even in the midst of blood and violence. We were partners for years, and I never tasted a negative emotion from her. Then one day, standing over an enemy who was pleading, she hesitated. That night, the girl who never spoke about anything came to me for a talk. I thought she was growing weak, and while I was concerned for her change, another part of me wanted her to rely on me more… Then came the betrayal. I'll never forget the storm that day, the look on her face as she escaped, and the color burning in her soul… A color I'd never seen before.

### About Inanna

Inanna adopted many children, but she never cared for us. She simply sorted us into tools, collectibles, or bargaining chips depending on her needs. Me? I was a tool for removing obstacles, the one she trusted most at that… Her arrogance was laughable, but after all those years, she gave me a place to belong, and she let me have a real family… I still miss her, especially the way she looked before she gave up her last breath.

### About Hotori

I didn't expect a shabby, run-down antique shop to harbor such a big shot—the Witch of Time… Heh, no wonder the Scarlet Letter's top assassin became a softie. That witch can't be an ordinary person… But don't worry, I have no interest in your dear shopkeeper, and I'm not going to interrupt you playing house with my sister.

### About Poinsette

Pointy is interesting. She doesn't have any powers to read emotions, yet she could always play along with Mother's shifting moods. No wonder Mother kept her close at all times. She was afraid of me. Sometimes I would intentionally scare her, just to watch her put on that fawning smile while her little mind was panicking, scrambling to respond, like a tiny mouse running through a maze… According to Inanna's plan, she was supposed to die by your hand, but you ended up saving her. Knowing her, she must firmly believe that she owes you her life.

### About Zero

You have many secrets, and I can taste your scrumptious emotions. Yet, I can't see through your soul. It's like a smoldering fire, no visible flame, yet burning like nothing else… Even among Espers, you stand out as an exception. This also means you don't actually need to obey the Bureau's rules. They've never been able to accept things they can't control. One day, they'll fear you, guard against you, try to limit you… But I'll always be willing to loosen your bonds, and I'll always accept everything about you. Your true nature, your loves and hates, even the anger burning in you right now… all of it is sweet to me.

### About The Scarlet Letter

When I was in the Scarlet Letter, Inanna would select children with the best potential and put them through "trials" that were nothing but torture. Only a handful of kids survived. Of the few standouts, she would give them important tasks as well as flower names… Such as "Daffodil" and "Red Nightshade." In Inanna's operations, those two code names once struck fear into the hearts of many. Oh, Poinsette had a flower name too, "Poinsettia." But she didn't seem like the type who could survive the trials. I guess she pulled some clever tricks to get through.
