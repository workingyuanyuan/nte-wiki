## Skills

Akane Rin builds String Energy with Stringblade and sets either single-target Overtone or area Reverb Field via Rhythm Wave to relay Basic Attacks, then spends 30 String Energy on Synced Resonance while using Sonic Boom's two-stage Solo Cadenza window.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Stringblade, Stage Dive, Critical Riposte: Featherblade Reverb | Stringblade performs up to four Lakshana attacks, generating String Energy up to 30 whenever Akane Rin deals damage. Stage Dive plunges from mid-air for area Lakshana DMG. Critical Riposte: Featherblade Reverb triggers after a Critical Dodge, dealing Lakshana DMG and preserving Stringblade's combo stage. |
| Skill | Rhythm Wave, Synced Resonance | Press Rhythm Wave to inflict Overtone on one target, or hold to deploy a Reverb Field; both relay Basic Attacks and Featherblade Reverb with bonus damage, and only one can exist. At 30 String Energy, cast Synced Resonance to consume it for massive damage while invincible. |
| Ultimate | Sonic Boom | Deals multi-hit area Lakshana DMG and enters Solo Cadenza with increased interruption resistance, independent of Overtone and Reverb Field. Can be manually recast once before Solo Cadenza expires to deal area damage and end the state early. Generates Ultimate Energy while out of combat. |
| Support Skill | Altered Chord | Strikes the strings with a powerful strum, dealing one instance of Lakshana DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Perfect Pitch | Remora Enhancement: After the team first triggers Remora, periodically applies Remora to targets with Overtone or inside Reverb Field until leaving combat. Reapplying Remora triggers extra Lakshana DMG counting as Redirect Skill DMG, scaling up with each target's remaining Remora duration. |
| Passive 2 | Starlit Anthem | Increases Akane Rin's ATK after casting Sonic Boom. This effect does not stack and resets its duration when triggered again. |
