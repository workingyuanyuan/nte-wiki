### Topic: Disqualified Spawn

It's not a big deal. When a human and an anomaly merge, if the human overcomes the anomaly, they become a Disqualified Spawn. If the anomaly overcomes the human, they become something even more inferior, living miserably… What's with that look?… Just to make it clear, I'm not about to be bested by those bottom-rung beings! So, if you're pitying me, you might as well step back.

### Topic: Empty Bottles

W…what? Empty milk bottles?! You must be seeing things… That's Kiroumaru's weird taste, not mine, definitely.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

How unlucky do you have to be to get stuck with someone like Kiroumaru? Always defiant and noisy! I can't count how many times I've cleaned up its mess because it eats everything! And when it gets an upset stomach, it vomits everywhere!

(Kiroumaru: Everything's on the menu! Gluttony rules!)

Zip—it—! One day I'll sew that mouth shut!

### About Myself: Wish

Curious, are we? Did those two meddlers say something?… Hmph. Save the guesswork. These lips are sealed.

### About Hotori

She's a lazy squanderer! Always stirring the pot and acting like a child! I was just showing my kindness to her everyday!

If Adler wasn't around, Eibon would've gone bankrupt ages ago! Taking care of the flaky bunch is a full-time gig. Why not care for yourself for a change?!

### About Daffodill

Daffodill's got moves, and I respect that. But teaming up with her? It's like a breath-holding contest with a statue. Ha… I bet she'd vibe with that dummy, Edgar!

### About Nanally

Psst, wanna see Nanally freak out? Put on a horror film, and she'll be hugging her tail in no time. Go on, dare her—but don't say I gave you the idea!

### About Edgar

Being a brainiac is great and all, but if he's too shy to speak up, what's the point? So yeah, I'm the one who steps up! But let me tell you, don't let that quiet act fool you. When it's go-time, he'll come out swinging and leave everyone's jaws on the floor!

### About the Coluccis

Ah, Nanally and the dummy, what a pair! One's scatterbrained, the other's tight-lipped. What would they do without me? But guess what? I… don't hate their company…

### About Taygedo

The dumbest oddity in the entire Bridge Crossings! Never understands the hierarchy! That dumb otter can only be my sidekick, okay? And he cries at the drop of a hat, so embarrassing! Hmm… but you know what, life would be boring without Taygedo… I guess he can stick around!

### About Mismo

Those self-righteous, high-and-mighty guys, I should have hit 'em harder last time! But I'm all about second chances, especially when they're trying to make things right. So, I'll let bygones be bygones… for now.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

Humph, I haven't found anywhere else that feels quite satisfying. Plus, Adler's cooking? That's reason enough to stay and lend a hand…
