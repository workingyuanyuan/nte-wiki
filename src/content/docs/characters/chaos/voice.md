## Daily

### First Meet

Greetings. This is Chaos from ETD-6. Pleasure to meet you. I'm looking forward to… Hey, I'm just trying to be formal, stop laughing!

### Chat: Mask

Why am I wearing a mask? I tell people I want to be more efficient in combat. But the real reason… are you sure you want to know? Well, I think it looks cool. Okay, stop staring at me like that. I really think it's cool…

### Chat: Troubling thing

Is the mission a lot of trouble? No need to be concerned. As long as you sort things out one at a time, you'll be able to succeed. This belief helped me do many things that seemed impossible at first, except for… well, never mind.

### Chat: Hound

I don't have a pet dog, but if you mean a "hound," then that's my Esper Ability. Hmm, maybe having a real dog as a pet would be nice…

### Sunny

My mood is as sunny as the sunshine!

### Rainy

Got an umbrella? Do you need my coat?

### Cold

Is it cold? You're shaking.

### Hot

I'm evaporating!

Good morning! A brand new day begins!

### Noon Greeting

It's time for a decision. What should I get for lunch?

### Evening Greeting

(Yawn)… I'm sleepy.

### Good Night

Good night. Sleep early, grow taller.

### Hobbies

Tetrominoes is so fun! I love it when the lines all vanish!

### Worries

Phones these days are too complicated. I just need a phone that can call, text, and… oh, play Tetrominoes!

### Topic: Scar

The body's self-healing powers are limited. It won't heal when it suffers too much damage. Such a shame.

### Topic: Weapon

I can use my abilities to create weapons with simple structures like swords and blades, but guns are tricky. It's not like I can't, but I'll have to remember all the components… Fine, I can't.

### Topic: Fortelling

I spent a few years in a place far from the city, where people lived by the "will of God." The will of God was always right, because people could always find a way to interpret it as right. Prophecies are much the same. If you find a good interpretation, then believe in it.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

My ability? It's shaping a mass of smoke from who knows where into all kinds of forms. Pretty pathetic compared to those that can change the course of nature, right? But because of that, I learned to rely on myself to get things done. That's not so bad!

### About Myself: Origins

In a small seaside town far away, a baby was born. He didn't know it then, but 27 years later, he'd grow into a 27-year-old fellow… What? I didn't say anything wrong, did I?

### About Myself: Farewell

Not to brag, but I've probably said my goodbyes more times than you've said good morning. Drifting out at sea, I'd always think the world has no real limits, so maybe the people I said goodbye to… I'll meet again.

### About Exe

The Captain only appears to be gloomy. He's actually a really good guy. When I first arrived, I didn't know how to use a smartphone. He walked me through it step by step. If he hadn't checked for me, I'd have woken up to 100 alarms on the next day. Just thinking about it gives me the chills!

### About Elyms

I've always had the feeling that he… was somebody that I used to know. I just couldn't figure out who he was. I asked him once whether he'd ever gone out to sea with me a few years ago. All he said was: "Have I?" I was dumbfounded. How can anyone forget that?

### About Linko

I don't know what's going on in Linko's brain. Well, that's not important. If you're looking for a person or an anomaly, you can simply trust her intuitions. The only problem is, she often finds herself in strange places. It's the same problem a little friend of mine had. Though frankly speaking, my friend is not as bright as Linko…

### About Daffodill

Daffodill blames herself too much, even for things out of her own control… She's impressive. She grew up in a less-than-ideal environment, but managed to stay kindhearted. The owner of Eibon… must be a great influence for her.

### About Zankou

She's strange. Almost as strange as criminals on Ghroth Island. To be precise, she belongs there.

### About Ghroth Island

At Ghroth Island… I worked, trained, honed my intuition into a technique, and learned how to tell good from evil. I appreciate them letting me stay there. Other than that, I don't have much personal feelings.

### About ETD-6

I can feel that ETD-6 isn't that unified of a team. Everyone has their own issues. Well, this isn't something I can ask of them. I just need them to do their jobs.
