The official profile describes Zankou as a dangerous Esper armed with a Cursed Blade: Inanna's adopted daughter and bodyguard, and the most feared assassin of The Scarlet Letter. Her Esper Ability, the Eye of Delusion, senses emotions and conjures illusions.

## Skills

Zankou alternates between Reality Form and Illusion Form via Sanguine Dash and Soulcross, applying Heartwrench and spreading DoTs as Follow-up Attack DMG. Timed Soulcross triggers Oblivion to activate Bloodfeast Reverie, which inflicts Vile Ash and grants a free enhanced Inferno Flamenco.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Wildfire, Nightmare Waltz, Flickering Shadow, Moonfall, Broken Twigs, Critical Riposte: Voidstep | In Reality Form, Wildfire and Flickering Shadow deal Incantation DMG and grant Hunt. In Illusion Form, Nightmare Waltz and Moonfall deal Follow-up Attack DMG, inflict Heartwrench, spread up to four DoT effects, and grant Delusion. Also includes the Broken Twigs plunge and Critical Riposte: Voidstep counter. |
| Skill | Sanguine Dash, Soulcross | In Reality Form, Sanguine Dash enters Illusion Form, inflicts Heartwrench, and triggers Disarray after specific combos to reduce Break. In Illusion Form, Soulcross returns to Reality Form as Follow-up Attack DMG, inflicts Heartwrench, and when enhanced before expiry triggers Oblivion to activate Bloodfeast Reverie. |
| Ultimate | Inferno Flamenco, Bloodfeast Reverie | Casts Inferno Flamenco when Bloodfeast Reverie is inactive. Prioritizes Bloodfeast Reverie when active, dealing Follow-up Attack DMG, inflicting Vile Ash across a large area, and enabling one enhanced Inferno Flamenco without consuming Ultimate Energy for a limited time. |
| Support Skill | Stoked Flame | Wields her blade to attack, dealing one instance of Incantation DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Silent Sunset | Applies Scorch Enhancement, allowing Scorch to stack up to three times. When allies inflict a DoT effect on a target with Scorch, Zankou adds one Scorch stack and replaces existing Scorch DMG, type, and duration with the new effect. |
| Passive 2 | Crimson Reverie | Sets Zankou's Cycle Energy to 100 upon entering battle once per battle and increases her Cycle Intensity by 100 while she is on the team. |
