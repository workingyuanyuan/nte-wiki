## Skills

Hotori accumulates energy over time on her Non-Closed Timepiece instead of using cooldowns or Ultimate energy. Her Skill spends 60 energy to record up to three ally Support or Redirect Skills, and her Ultimate triggers Time Stop to replay them and unleash katana combos.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Misty Moon Style, Non-Closed Timepiece, Rippling Waves, Crescent, Critical Riposte: Half Moment | Misty Moon Style executes up to five Cosmos attacks, Rippling Waves dashes forward when held in combat, and Crescent plunges from midair. Non-Closed Timepiece passively rotates when Hotori switches in to store up to 120 energy. Critical Riposte: Half Moment counters after a Critical Dodge to reduce Break. |
| Skill | Present Replay | Has no cooldown; spends 60 Non-Closed Timepiece energy when above 60 to deal seven Cosmos DMG hits, rewind the pointer, and record up to three ally Support or Redirect Skills (one per ally). Entering or leaving battle clears records, and recasting overwrites previous records. |
| Ultimate | World's Tide | Has no cooldown or Ultimate Energy cost; casts at full Non-Closed Timepiece energy for ten Cosmos hits, entering Time Stop with a katana while disabling switching and granting time-control immunity. Replays recorded attacks once, builds katana combo stacks to ten for Finisher DMG, and rewinds the pointer. |
| Support Skill | Shopkeeper's Authority | Throws her umbrella to deal one instance of Cosmos DMG to an area. |
| Passive 1 | Ghost Orchid Crest | Provides a Blossom Enhancement that prevents Vita Bud attacks from pausing during Time Stop. |
| Passive 2 | All Treasures Under Heaven | Increases World's Tide's Finisher DMG Ratio. |
