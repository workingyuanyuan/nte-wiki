## Daily

### First Meet

Beep-boop, beep-boop—Calling the Appraiser, this is Linko from ETD-6, currently working on operation "Let's Hang Out." Please respond… or give me a nod.

### Chat: Field Duty

The next section to patrol is… Umm… I'll just go with the flow.

### Chat: Movies

Appraiser, Appraiser! There's a new paranormal movie at Floe Cinema! Wanna see it together?

### Chat: Hethereau

Hethereau is a pretty accepting city. I run into all kinds of fun stuff on my patrols, and the citizens are used to strange things happening from time to time.

### Sunny

They say that if you let the sun shine on your face, you'll get this warm and fuzzy feeling, like a cat whisking its tail on your face…

### Rainy

I really love the sound of rain falling on empty soda cans… if I lined up a few cans with different pitches, could I compose a song?

### Cold

So cold… If only Xiaozhen's ghost fire could warm me up…

(Xiaozhen：Huh? How can I do that? I'm not some scorching spirit!)

### Hot

It's so warm… If I wrapped Xiaozhen around my neck, I'd feel a lot better… Xiao… zhen…

(Xiaozhen：No way! Not even a ghost will leave her TV in this hot weather!)

### Morning Greeting

Morning, Appraiser… Oof… I went to bed late last night, hah…

(Xiaozhen: The little scamp stayed up late again. Even ghosts go to bed earlier…)

### Noon Greeting

Appraiser, Appraiser, are dreams from naps different from the ones you have at night? Do dreams work like movies in theaters, with matinee showings and late-night showings?

### Evening Greeting

The sun's setting. Soon, the city's many signals will slowly quiet down, just like the white foam of the waves hissing away as the tide returns to the sea.

### Good Night

The universe sings a lullaby every night, sometimes it's the stars, sometimes it's the moon… Appraiser, if only you could hear it too.

### Hobbies

I got this big cabinet at home stuffed with movie discs. Each night, I pick one randomly and watch it with Xiaozhen. I love alien and paranormal stuff, but Xiaozhen hates them. She always says why bother watching fake special effects when she, an actual spirit, is the real deal…

(Xiaozhen: Hmph! If they ever cast this evil spirit as the antagonist, I'll make a movie that's a thousand times scarier!)

### Worries

Worries… I've never thought about any. If I must say, I guess I don't have the answers to certain questions. Like… where to catch a shiny Tamachi, why Exe is always mad at Elyms, how to get Xiaozhen to admit she's cute, what the radio waves from deep space actually sound like… and, whether people tuned to different frequencies can really understand each other… so many questions, so few answers.

### Topic: Frequency

The world is like a radio station. Humans, anomalies, and everything else all broadcast their unique signal on their private channels. Whenever I pick up an interesting signal, I go look for where it came from… Usually, the stranger the signal, the more surprising its source turns out to be: like a philosophical water bird standing by the river, contemplating life; or some local resident standing in a wheat field with a pot on their head, trying to summon aliens; or a location anomaly that hates being alone and craves visitors.

### Topic: Headset

The Bureau gave me this customized headset. I think it's called… the "Static Sifter Headset"? I turn this dial, and it clears out the noise, letting me focus and search for a specific frequency, kind of like a radio. It also doubles as regular headphones. I use it to listen to rock music from anomalies, compilations of oddity calls, or white noise from anomalous realms… stuff like that. The sound quality's great.

(Xiaozhen：What in Hethereau do you listen to…)

### Topic: Tamachi

Tamachi GO is a game about electronic pets. It used to be insanely popular. Tons of players flooded the streets to catch pets and battle. Not a lot of people play it these days… Heheh, In fact, before you stands one of Hethereau's top five Tamachi masters—just one step away from catching them all—well, counting shiny ones and variants, more like—three or four steps away!

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

I can sense the frequencies of the people around me. If I focus and listen closely to one of them, there's this resonance that gradually sharpens, until at some point, everything feels transparent and encompassing, like a heartbeat perfectly in sync, linking me straight to the source of that signal, understanding everything, sharing everything—no more misunderstandings or barriers in the way… I long for that feeling, even just for a fleeting moment.

### About Myself: Battle

I hope the way Xiaozhen and I fight alongside each other doesn't scare you. Those big hands are actually Xiaozhen's. As for how we make it work… if I'm synced enough with a certain frequency, sometimes I can fully resonate with its source… It's like we share emotions, feelings, powers, and act as one… It feels like some kind of power-up state? It's hard to keep that going though. Right now, Xiaozhen's the only one I can keep a stable resonance with…

(Xiaozhen：Huh? Am I not enough for you? Why don't you go ask those baddies you arrested about how I scared the heck outta them! Can't believe this scamp wants more, humph…)

### About Myself: Family

Both my mom and dad are Espers. Dad's power is sinking into the couch for a whole day and binging through movies quietly. Mom's power is talking nonstop while walking through a dozen streets without needing rest. I inherited both of their powers, but maybe because they are total opposites, I just got a watered-down power. Compared to them, I just have an ordinary love for movies and going out for fun…

### About Myself: Work

Unit-6's job… simply put, is catching people who use anomalies or their Esper Ability to do bad things. My day-to-day is using my powers to search for baddies and track them down. Usually, Exe shares with me intel about a target, like where they've been spotted, aliases they've used, or what websites they've browsed, and what kind of music they like, stuff like that. Once I get a feel, I can figure out what their frequencies sounds like, then I just go around the city listening for where they're coming from… How does it actually work? Um… I don't know, it's basically just… going with the vibe?

### About Nitsa

Nitsa's frequency is super regular, like… a piano that follows the music sheet's every detail. Sometimes it speeds up, until it's hard to catch your breath even if you just listen. But she's extremely reliable! She can recite the entire ETD standard procedure manual word for word. The problem is… she always wants to follow the process step by step and do everything by the book. When she runs into something unexpected, she tends to freak out. But don't you worry, as Nitsa's senpai, I'll take care of her!

(Xiaozhen: You know, you started like half a year ahead of her…)

### About Chaos

Frequency from Chaos tends to be noisy—it goes on and on, just like his chatters. His channel only goes quiet when he faces Esper criminals. In those times, his signal rumbles and growls, almost like some wild beast… Though we team up for many missions, we often end up splitting up because he wanders off while tailing a target… I mean, obviously he's the one who gets lost when I'm the one on the right path.

(Xiaozhen: If I were Chaos, I'd grab your ahoge and pull it off right now.)

### About Elyms

Big sis Elyms… I'm so happy to see her again. Her frequencies are completely different from before. They're now like a bunch of unstable channels stacked on top of each other. It's hard for me to hear clearly… When we met again, I thought she was someone else, but she recognized me right away. She even remembered taking me to catch Tamachis. She still likes to pinch my cheeks, just like back then… I guess people's frequencies can change too.

### About Exe

Exe likes to shut himself away in the server room. It's pretty hard to see him in person. When he does show up, he always has this stern and intimidating look, but he's actually a gentle person. His frequency is almost hypnotic, like listening to rain through a thick window… or that steady, rapid clatter of someone typing on the keyboard. But sometimes… well, mostly when he's around big sis Elyms, I hear these scary noises from him and they scare me a bit… Anyway, I hope they get along some day.

### About Xiaozhen

The first time I met Xiaozhen was deep inside an anomalous realm. Back then, I picked up a frequency that felt like… a CRT TV screen that was just switched off—warm but with some static. I listened as I searched for the source, then I found an old TV. Once I plugged it in, Xiaozhen appeared on the screen, curled up like a stray cat sleeping in a tattered cardboard box… Yep, it's the little TV I carry on my back, that's her home, and I'm the one who carries her home around. That makes me her family.

### About ETD-6

You want to know more about us? Let me think… Actually, Exe does smile. Sometimes when we talk about old games, he gets this nostalgic smile, but then he sinks right back into gloom… Big sis Elyms is very kind, but sometimes I catch her telling Exe things too difficult for me to understand. I really hope she's not bullying the captain… As for Chaos, he always says Dally is nothing but a construct he created with his power, but honestly, he treats the hound really well… As for me, umm… Oh right, I'm actually an alien.
