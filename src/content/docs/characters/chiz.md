The official profile introduces Chiz as the Lobby Manager of Pink Paws Bank (New Herland District HQ), employee ID 018888, who looks like an intern until she recites every clause of the fine print.

Game8 spells her Skill "Pink Paws Priority Principe"; Prydwen spells it "Pink Paws Priority Principle". Version 1.1 gave Chiz a Life Skill that highlights all "Pink Paws Heist" collectibles on the map.

## How to obtain

Chiz is free. Game8 says she is obtained by raising the City Tycoon level; Prydwen lists her, and her extra copies, as obtainable without spending.

## Skills

Chiz generates Grain through Basic Attacks and spends it via stacked Redirect Skill casts, using her Ultimate's Grain Market and Surplus state to convert Grain Loans and fluctuating Grain Prices into Cosmos DMG.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Exiled Swordplay, Barren, Bountiful Grain, Critical Riposte: Minsky Moment | Exiled Swordplay strikes up to five times for Cosmos DMG and Grain. Barren taps or holds Blighted Vale for Cosmos DMG, Grain, and Fons (bonus on defeat). Bountiful Grain plunges midair for Grain, and Critical Dodge enables Critical Riposte: Minsky Moment, reducing Break and granting Grain. |
| Skill | Pink Paws Priority Principle | Stores up to three Redirect Skill stacks. Consecutive presses cast No Grain, Half Grain, and Full Grain; holding spends two or three stacks, continuously draining Grains during Half Grain and spending all Grains and Debt-state Grain Loans for non-crit bonus Cosmos DMG, enhanced in Surplus. |
| Ultimate | Zero-Sum Game | Throws Brown Sugar Boba for AoE Cosmos DMG, converts Grain Loans into Grains, enters Surplus and Grain Market, and invests all Grains. Guaranteed Crit on the first Grain Settlement DMG; pressing or holding Pink Paws Priority Principle triggers Grain Settlement via Grain Price, repaying Grain Loans afterward. |
| Support Skill | Temporary Entry | Brown Sugar Boba carries Chiz and crashes into the target, dealing one instance of Cosmos DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Hanahaki Disease | Charge Enhancement grants active characters additional Charge. |
| Passive 2 | Hoarding | Increases Chiz's Charge Efficiency while she is on the field. |

## Awakenings

Each duplicate unlocks one Awakening of the player's choice; Resonances unlock at 3 and 6 Awakenings.

| Awakening | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Sun, Sun… speak to me! | Increases the storage capacity of Grain and Grain Loan by 10%. |
| Fireworks in Silence | Grants 10% of current Grain as interest per second while Chiz is in the Surplus state. |
| If There Were No Rainy Days | Increases the chance of a Grain Price increase by 50%. |
| To Riceball, Bandage, Fons | Increases Grain acquisition efficiency by 20%. |
| Blank Diary Pages | Grants Chiz Grain equal to 100% of the Grain storage limit upon entering combat. |
| Thirty-Three Days | Increases the Bonus Cosmos DMG of Grain by 15%. |
| Resonance 3: Friends and Family | Increases Grain acquisition efficiency by an extra 50% while in the Debt state. |
| Resonance 6: Homecoming | Reduces the target's Cosmos Resistance by 10% upon Chiz dealing damage. |
