## Daily

### First Meet

Hi, my name is Aurelia… Oh, is this one scaring you? Sorry, it gets excited when it meets a new friend… I hope you don't mind. Hmm? You're not scared? Great!

### Chat: Advice

When playing the drums, never wear wide-leg pants! If you hit the pedal too hard, the pants can easily get caught in the bass drum beater…

### Chat: Night Out

I like going out at night. There aren't a lot of people on the streets, so I don't have to worry about bumping into anyone. Though if they saw me floating on the road that late, they might get spooked…

### Chat: Music

Every music genre has its own charm, and every song carries the unique emotions of its creator. But if you're asking for my top band… I can't pick just one. Let me put together a playlist instead!

### Hobbies

Oh, there's a lot! I love trying all sorts of new things, though I tend to lose interest quickly… If there's one thing that's stayed with me, it would have to be gazing at the sea. The distant line where the sky meets the water and the gentle rhythm of the waves… puts my heart at ease, as if I'm a tiny note in a grand and eternal choir… All my worries fade away when I gaze at the sea.

### Worries

My go-to navigation app has been updating lately and keeps glitching out. Now, I get lost all the time. I've shown up late to my part-time job many times this week… My kind boss hasn't docked my pay, but I still feel bad about it… I might just get a paper map until the app gets fixed.

### Topic: Jellyfish

Every now and then, you'll find jellyfish washed up on the shore, left behind by the retreating tide. Perhaps they've lost their way too… If you ever come across a jellyfish on the beach, please don't touch it. They may look soft and harmless, but many of them are venomous. Oh, the ones around me are completely harmless though, so don't worry!

### Topic: Dangerous Driving

This one is just too large to fit on most forms of transportation. Fortunately, it's fast, so even when there's something urgent, not being able to board a vehicle isn't an issue… Hm? You're asking how fast? W-Well, let's just say, not fast enough to get lectured by Officer Whiskers for speeding through the city on a jellyfish.

### Topic: Anomaly

If I hadn't taken on the mission to nullify Swallowtail that day, perhaps none of this would have happened. That anomaly was just as dangerous as it was beautiful… I know you're a very capable Anomaly Hunter, but please take good care of yourself. Not just for your own sake, but for the people who care about you and love you.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

My ability is rather weak. I can only control jellyfish. But thanks to these little ones, I was able to return to a normal life. When I'm all by myself, they keep me company, so I never feel lonely. That's why I'm truly grateful to have an ability like this. It's just like they say, "What suits you is what's best for you." It's that simple.

### About Myself: Rehabilitation

The doctor said I'll likely never make a full recovery. But if I keep up with rehab and get medical treatment, one day I might be able to stand again. You might think it's nothing more than a fleeting wish, and that I'm foolish for holding on to it… but I believe that before life reaches its end, anything is possible. If a miracle were to come, and I wasn't ready for it, wouldn't that be a shame?

### About Myself: Delicacies

There are many great eateries along Tamamochi Street where I work. The roast duck at Din's is always crispy on the outside and tender on the inside; Nekomaru Ramen is wonderful, but you need to watch out for the hot broth. My favorite is the custard buns from Bigmouth Baozi. I love holding one in my hands on cold days, then taking a bite to to warm, fragrant sweetness… it's pure bliss. Though if I eat too many and put on weight, I might burden this little one… I suppose I'll have to control myself.

### About Akane

Thank you for supporting Akane's return to the stage. It means a great deal to me as well. You're a drummer, too. I'm sure you understand. Akane is always so shiny on stage when I look from behind. It's as if she was born to perform… From now on, I'll keep on cheering for The Whoots!!!! Don't forget I've been your number one fan since your very first gig!

### About LINES

The truth is, LINES wasn't born out of a desire to start a band. I wanted to find a reason for the three of us to stay together after we graduated. Perhaps that's why, when I could no longer play, LINES just fell apart… But the connections between people aren't just straight lines; even when one intersection is lost, there's always a chance to meet again. I wish I understood that sooner… but it's not too late, even now.
