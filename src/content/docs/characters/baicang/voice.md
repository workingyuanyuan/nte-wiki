## Daily

### First Meet

We've been through life and death together, so let's skip all the formalities, shall we? Hmm… but, come to think of it, I think I've never properly introduced myself… Captain, ETD-4—The name's Baicang. Let's continue getting along well, shall we?

### Chat: Relaxation

Being serious is a virtue, sure, but tensing up all the time is more exhausting than it ever needed to be! Here's some advice as a friend—you should loosen up once in a while!

### Chat: Pranks

Wondering why I'm out here? Uh… as you can see, I've been locked outside by Fadia and Mosa. Sigh… How could they be so heartless to their own Captain… Hm? "What did you do this time?" Hey, you're being a little unfair to me, don't you think? Even you assume it's my fault? I mean, I did draw a mustache on Mosa's face while she was sleeping… but! Fadia already made us even! Yet they still locked me outside after that! That can't be a fair trial and punishment…

### Chat: Invitation

Looks like you're having a good time in Eibon. Seems like she likes you enough. But… if you ever fancy a change of scenery, our doors at ETD are always open for you.

### Sunny

Why waste such a beautiful day cooped up in the office doing paperwork? Yup! I'll deal with that later.

### Rainy

If you're looking to kill time, try watching roof tiles in the rain. It's quite fascinating. They shimmer differently than usual, with water droplets bouncing from different angles, creating sounds of different frequencies… almost like someone is trying to whisper something.

### Cold

I proposed introducing "days off for cold weather" for my squad. Which passed unanimously… except for Skia's vote. Then, our dear Director rejected it. Ah well, if you've got any "cold weather days off" or "winter breaks" where you're from, do enjoy them on my behalf.

### Hot

To reward myself for my diligent effort working under this scorching heat! I'm planning to take a detour and buy some ice cream from Blizzi. Shh, don't tell Skia… Hm? Hey, this isn't slacking off! But, as thanks for keeping my secret, I'll grab one for you too! So, what flavor would you like?

### Morning Greeting

Morning! Such a lovely day, perfect for taking it easy… Ah, better not let them find me though. See ya.

### Noon Greeting

Perfect timing. I was just about to grab some tea during my lunch break. Care to join me?

### Evening Greeting

Smooth sailing 'til meal time! …Unfortunately, I've got an assignment soon… so dinner better be quick and simple.

### Good Night

Getting sleepy? Oh… I suppose it is that time of night already. I got so caught up in our chat that I kept you here for too long without realizing. My bad, my bad.

### Hobbies

Don't you ever wonder what kind of face people would make if they found me playing dead in the break room during lunch break? Hahaha! Actually, I'm gonna try it in a bit. Why don't you become the discoverer of my "body"?

### Worries

Care less, worry less! Hah, don't give me that look! I never said I had no worries. Let me keep some secrets to myself for now, alright?

### Topic: Communication

They say language is vessels of the mind, but when people want to convey something, people will always find ways to express themselves, Tower of Babel or not. Words, actions, expressions… Don't you find interpreting them fascinating? Say, look—Skia's expression right now is… uh, he found out I skipped work yesterday…

### Topic: Day Off

Vacations are all about following your whims and making spontaneous plans! For me—sipping tea, checking out that new cake shop, trading gardening tips with the florist, catching up with old friends… Oh, and there's this beautiful forest near the cemetery at the foot of the mountain. In the evening, when the breeze flows over your skin… It feels good.

### Topic: Regrets

Sometimes I wonder, if everything that's gone loses its history and name, what remains? The ideas they had, the emotions they experienced… Where do they go? Would a late sun apologize to a withered sunflower…?

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

Words hold far more power than people realize. Scary, isn't it? Haha… With desire, even the most ordinary words can easily bring suffering or happiness. But, I'm sure you know how to use this power wisely.

### About Myself: Past

The past? It's rare to meet someone as curious as you. Hmm… where should I begin? How about this: Long, long ago, a young man found a creature by the sea that should only have existed in myths… What later generations would consider the earliest anomaly in Hethereau… Hm? Not the past you were looking for?

### About Myself: Captain

Titles like "Captain" don't really mean much anyway. Me, I'm not one for putting on airs, and my team? They aren't going to respect me more just because I have some title. If you ask me, being captain is nothing but a hassle—speaking of which, I need to come up with an excuse to make Skia attend the Bureau's meeting tomorrow morning in my place…

### About Alphard

At the rate the Director's docking my pay… Sooner or later, I'll need a loan just to come to work. Ahh… Now I'm wondering when she will actually kick me out.

### About Skia

Don't let Skia's scary face fool you, he takes his work very seriously. Haha, having such a reliable Lieutenant makes my job as Captain much easier. Though, it would be nice if he wasn't so strict about my little slip-ups.

### About Fadia

Never mess with Fadia unless you're prepared, take it from someone who knows better. Though, if you're feeling confident, by all means! Try your luck, and promise you'd let me watch! Curious about what happened to me? Heh… let's not get into that.

### About Lacrimosa

Mosa's incredibly talented, but she's still just a kid who doesn't know much about the world. As an adult, I should look after her. Ugh, why does she hate me so much though? It really breaks my heart…

### About Hotori

She sometimes reminds me of the Director—just as impossible to figure out… Though she seems different now compared to back then? Maybe she's been drinking too much?

### About Bureau of Anomaly Control

The Bureau wouldn't be half bad without all those fancy rules and stuffy traditionalists. Though, I guess there wouldn't be much left after removing all that, would there?

### About ETD

ETD is just a bunch of enigmatics doing whatever they want. They look good on the news, but working with them is nothing but headaches. Everyone showing off their special talents when they're together… it's more like a circus than a workplace. Though, I'll admit it is pretty entertaining… "You're part of it"? Ahaha, why, thank you!

### About ETD-4

Squad 4's Captain is being isolated by his team members…? Who said that? That's not true! We're known throughout the Bureau as a model team with unparalleled unity… Wait, you saw Mosa, Fadia, and Skia hanging out together near the plaza for shopping and dinner? Huh? When did that happen?
