## Daily

### First Meet

Lieutenant Skia, Squad 4, Enforcement Tactical Division. Perhaps we might meet at work again in the future.

### Chat: Work Environment

Whether in studies or work, everyone is deeply influenced by their environment and unconsciously becomes part of it—it goes both ways.

### Chat: Pace of Life

You came to this city without any prior knowledge or preparation, so I imagine Eibon will shape your life here. What do you think? Is this life for you?

### Chat: Meeting

If you see Baicang, could you please ask him to reply to my message as soon as possible? I've organized the meeting minutes he missed and sent them to his phone. We need him to GO THROUGH them by today.

### Sunny

When the sun is directly overhead, it's harder for my shadow to spread from beneath my feet. However… within short distances, the condensed and distinct shadow packs a way stronger punch.

### Rainy

The humid weather will significantly impact outdoor operations, and it's not just about slippery surfaces. Make sure to watch out for anomalies that adapt their patterns based on weather changes.

### Cold

I'm not cold… but don't count on me as a heat source.

### Hot

This weather makes me irritable, but I'm not a dog—you won't catch me panting to cool down!

### Morning Greeting

Good morning. Where should we start today?

### Noon Greeting

The food at the Bureau's cafeteria is neither good nor bad. The orderly queuing and dining system is certainly efficient, but it's only natural to get tired of eating there every day.

### Evening Greeting

ETD doesn't have fixed off-duty hours—we're expected to be ready for deployment at any time. Don't hesitate to call me whenever something comes up.

### Good Night

You've worked hard today. Get some rest.

### Hobbies

It's hard to say I have any particular hobbies. If I had to choose, I suppose I prefer… the feeling of seeing all my tasks progressing steadily toward completion.

### Worries

Troubles? …With Baicang or Fadia? …Well, it's understandable that Lacrimosa acts out sometimes and causes minor disturbances… But those two are adults slacking off and causing problems. It's hard not to get a headache from it.

### Topic: Anomaly - Ms. Fei

Ms. Fei's coffee has… a unique flavor, but it keeps you alert. I don't mind it. She gets upset if we brew tea at our desks, so we have to go outside for that.

### Topic: Anomaly - Agent.LUCK

Agent.LUCK handles all the paperwork nobody else at the Bureau wants to do. Tedious and endless as it may be. It persists in the belief that its master will return home once everything is complete… Watching it work diligently reminds me of that staff member who vanished into the lights and remains missing to this day, and of all the unresolved cases we still haven't found a way to tackle…

### Topic: Resume

Yes, I worked for quite a while at a company… one you might have heard of. Then I spent some time working with some outlaws, and now I serve in a public institution. Some say my resume is rather unusual, but looking back, perhaps it was all just trial and error in search of where I belong.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

Are you wondering why my shadow seems to have a different temperament from me, even though I'm the one controlling it…? It might be a bit abstract to explain. Let's just say that after awakening my Esper Ability, and as I gained a deeper understanding of my role, I realized I didn't need to maintain a tense facade all the time. That's when my shadow started appearing more energetic than before.

### About Myself: Animorphic

It's quite common for Esper ability awakenings to come with transfer characteristics, but as you can see, my physical transformation is far more severe than most. That day, I tried my best to stay calm, attempting to maintain my usual routine with rationality and courtesy. But inevitably, I lost my place among the usual "elites"… I suppose that was the "price" I had to pay.

### About Myself: "Elite"

After that, I did go through a period where it felt like the foundation I'd built my life on had crumbled… But it's fine now—I've long since stopped caring about that job and the standards it set for me.

### About Alphard

The director is a leader of great resolve. However, she doesn't particularly mind how her subordinates conduct themselves, rather focusing solely on results… Perhaps that's why the ETD can afford to be so lenient with its many free spirits…

### About Baicang

When Baicang says he's "exhausted", "done for", or "gravely injured"… don't waste your energy on him… If he speaks, he's alive enough. Talking from experience.

### About Fadia

One time… field duty with Fadia… I ran into an old acquaintance from the past. We chatted for a bit… the guy asked me if "I am working an honest job or not"… Thinking about how colleagues' public image can greatly influence others' perception of the workplace and the job… I'm tired of this…

### About Lacrimosa

If I don't pick up Lacrimosa or meet her early… she might not even be allowed on site because she doesn't have her ID. Compared to that, oversleeping or getting lost are very minor concerns.

### About Bureau of Anomaly Control

I used to think the ideal path for me was a stable management position in a large corporation, living a perfectly ordered life. I never imagined I'd get involved with an organization like the bureau that handles emergencies. Life is unpredictable—a series of unexpected turns brought me here, and now this daily routine of handling the unexpected has gradually become my new sense of order…

### About ETD

In the past, I would have had little tolerance for subordinates who lacked team spirit or were too much of an individual. But now, here… those who can't accept working with such spirits are probably the ones who don't fit in.
