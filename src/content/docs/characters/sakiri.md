## Profile

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class | S-Class, standard |
| Attribute | Incantation |
| Arc type | Gas |
| Joined | Version 1.0 |

The official profile introduces Sakiri as the author of the sticky-note rules on the fridge at Eibon Antique Shop. She wields the Anomaly war hammer Kiroumaru.

## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair, according to Game8 and Prydwen |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan. How Boards work: [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Skills

Sakiri is an Incantation support who pulls and controls enemies with Devour Whole and Feast of Gluttony while boosting Team ATK based on her Base ATK. Her passives lower enemy DEF after Airborne or Supress and amplify DoT taken under Scorch.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Kiroumaru Headbutt, Ram, Eating Mode, Swallow, Critical Riposte: Mischief | Performs up to five Incantation attacks with Ram, an aerial plunge with Swallow, and a counter strike reducing Break with Critical Riposte: Mischief after a Critical Dodge. Holding activates Eating Mode to pull nearby targets while Hungry and devour weak enemies until becoming Full. |
| Skill | Devour Whole, Burp, Gravity Eater | Press to activate Burp, pulling in nearby enemies before inflating Kiroumaru to knock them down. Hold to activate Gravity Eater, pulling in nearby enemies and devouring gravity in the area to levitate them, though this does not affect powerful enemies. |
| Ultimate | Feast of Gluttony | Kiroumaru spits Gravity Slurry from above for six area Incantation hits, pressing enemies down with strong gravity and stopping Suppressed enemies from moving (ineffective against Bosses). Also increases Team ATK, excluding Sakiri, based on Sakiri's Base ATK. |
| Support Skill | Squash! | Slams the enlarged Kiroumaru onto the target, dealing one instance of Incantation DMG to an area. |
| Passive 1 | Can I Eat This? | Applies Scorch Enhancement, increasing the DoT damage taken by targets in the Scorch state for each type of DoT effect on them, up to a cap. |
| Passive 2 | Impish Trick | Reduces enemies' DEF after Sakiri inflicts Airborne or Supress. |
