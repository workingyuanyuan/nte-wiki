## Daily

### First Meet

Hello, I'm Daffodill, Hotori's bodyguard… Too simple? Sorry, small talk isn't my strong suit. If you need anything, just reach out to Eibon, the antique shop.

### Chat: Rest

You need to do something. Sitting idle won't do your body any good.

### Chat: Weapons

Even the best weapons need regular maintenance to stay sharp, and so do people.

### Chat: Greetings

Dear customer… wel… welcome… Tch.

### Sunny

Good weather. Time for some outdoor training.

### Rainy

It's raining? I can use rain to mask my presence…

### Cold

The cold stiffens the body and numbs the senses… In such a moment, perhaps it's best for the roles of prey and hunter to be reversed.

### Hot

Argh… this temperature is annoying.

### Morning Greeting

Good morning, what's the plan for today?

### Noon Greeting

If you're tired, you can take a nap. I'll wake you up.

### Evening Greeting

We can train a bit more. Want to join?

### Good Night

Rest well, Eibon is safe at night.

### Hobbies

I can't say I have any particular preferences, just survival needs. Compressed sweets are easy to carry and provide a lot of energy, so I bring some just in case. How they taste doesn't really matter.

### Worries

Some customers are harder to deal with than anomalies, but Hotori says we should always smile for Eibon's business… Huh? You think our commission success rate has been dropping lately? Ahem… that's absolutely not true.

### Topic: Anomalies

Truth often hides among lies. Both humans and anomalies are not what they seem on the surface. Merely "seeing" does not suffice. You must understand, perceive, and analyze. Only when you adopt a kaleidoscope of perspectives… can you fathom truth itself.

### Topic: Training

I don't find all the daily training tedious. No pain, no gain, right? The only way to avoid screwing up again is by getting stronger.

### Topic: Fishing

I've observed those anglers who sit by the shore all day with nothing to show for it. The weather, water temperature, conditions of the fish—so many uncontrollable factors can lead to failure. But patience is always a quality a good hunter should have. Your bucket is going to end up full eventually.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

Just a trade with some of my vision, doesn't affect my daily life too much. Sometimes you hear more than you see.

### About Myself: Dark Colors

Wondering why I wear dark clothes all the time…? It's not because I like them, but because they do a better job at hiding my injuries and deceiving my enemies.

### About Myself: Name

It seems to mean "narcissus." Only one flower blooms on each stem. Hotori told me there's one in the lake near my place, though I've never seen it. If possible, I'd prefer you to call me Daffodill instead of…ahem.

### About Hotori: Concerns

I know Hotori will do whatever it takes to get what she wants, putting herself at risk along the way. But sometimes the way she goes about it is a bit over-the-top… Well, I guess I do have some catching up to do.

### About Hotori: First Meeting

She was already drunk the first time we met. I thought she was just another strange woman. But somehow… there was a sense of calm, a strange… trust. Maybe that's why I stayed.

### About Nanally

That kid's always peeking at me when I train. I've caught her a few times, but she just finds another spot and keeps at it… Well, as long as she doesn't get in my way, I guess.

### About Zankou

She's very dangerous. Avoid any contact with her unless necessary.

### About Adler

Adler is a very respectable person. Before I met Hotori, he was always by her side. I have a lot to learn from him.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

Eibon is completely different from where I used to be. Here, you have to deal with difficult customers for a living, and your friends here are noisy all the time… but I can sleep here without worrying about anything at least.

### Character Ascension I

Hard work pays off.

### Character Ascension II

I can be stronger.

### Character Ascension III

This way, your safety is guaranteed.

### Gift Received I

I don't think I've told you, but I… like it a lot. Thank you.

### Gift Received II

This is precious for me. Many thanks.

### Gift Received III

Thanks for the effort.

### Gift Received IV

Hotori told me I should gladly receive gifts from a friend. I shall receive it… gladly.
