## Daily

### First Meet

Sterry Express at your service. Nice to meet you, you can call me Jiuyuan. We provide anomaly hunter delivery service, and also accept anomaly commissions. If those are what you come for, we can have some further discussion.

### Chat: Sports Car Delivery

Behavior, manner, dressing… Tiny things manipulate people's attitude towards you more than you expect. Dealing with bussiness driving a Regalia seems to be showing off, but always makes way for you, so that things will go smoothly.

### Chat: Personal Food Survey

In the aroma of fine dishes and wine, a casual chat can reveal a lot more than people think… Of course, I'm not there for work every time. Sitting down and savoring the moment isn't a bad thing to do sometimes.

### Chat: Job Details

I'm in special deliveries for Sterry. The content… depends on the client. Different people have different needs.

### Sunny

Delivering on a sunny day is always better than a gloomy one, don't you think?

### Rainy

By yourself on a rainy day? Need a ride?

### Cold

It's never easy working in this weather for me, as well as you anomaly hunters… However, we both know that troubles bring you chances.

### Hot

People get more impatient under hot weather… Don't make them wait too long.

### Morning Greeting

Our first client this morning! What would you like to know?

### Noon Greeting

Ah. The high noon sun. Why don't we chat under some shades away from the blinding light?

### Evening Greeting

People tend to let their guard down in the evening… Are you paying attention to your surroundings?

### Good Night

Nightfall… the perfect time to negotiate.

### Hobbies

Nothing special. Just slowing down when I have the time. Drink some coffee, look at some people around, and pick up some interesting news.

### Worries

Troubles… probably those monthly parking tickets, damaged vases and decors at Sterry, and that limited edition perfume spilled out of curiosity… You think there are other things keeping me busy? Sharp eyes you have.

### Topic: Intel

I don't rush to judgement on intel. I see who is in it, who is into it… Focus on the line and wait, those who hide deeply but always concern will bite the hook.

### Topic: Reading Silence

Silence is sometimes harder to handle than noise. It hides hesitation, fear, concealment… and truths that don't want to be revealed. For intel merchants, the challenge isn't listening, but discerning. What's worth pursuing, what should be ignored. Miss the mark, and those opportunities that should have been seized will quietly slip away.

### Topic: Cheek Pouches

Every Sterry member has their own Cheek Pouch. These little guys are influenced by their contractor's personality. Observing their behavior is quite interesting sometimes.

### About Myself: Esper Ability

My esper? Consider it like turning roses to bullets when necessary. To achieve a goal, there's no harm to use a direct approach.

### About Myself: Rose

The bullet that shot him was roses he gave me not long ago. What a pity… for the roses.

### About Myself: Nightingale

Smart birds know when to get lost.

### About Jenson

As one of Sterry's founders, his intel abilities are naturally impeccable. If only he could spend less time using "intel gathering" as an excuse for slacking off, and spend more time fulfilling his duties as shop owner, and maintain relationship with some VICs, AND deal with those pending high risk commissions… There comes my turn to have a vacation. After all, there's nothing worth "investigating" around the Caltrop Lake, is there?

### About Hathor

She always does things at her own pace, carrying out instructions. I just have her observe how others laugh, make noise, and mess up ordinary little things. These trivial moments are more important to her than any training.

### About Haniel

Looking for her to make deliveries? Open your Bagel, ask a few simple questions… Most of the time she'll show up and won't keep you waiting. As for causing trouble, she handles big things appropriately. For small matters, just watch if she's being overly helpful around the shop. That usually means she's making up for some minor mishap, quietly cleaning up the mess.

### About Illica

Illica… his problem isn't causing trouble but insisting on being smarter than everyone else. Especially when there's someone his age around, his little brain starts sparking. He's genuinely clever, but unfortunately uses it all for making trouble.

### About Sterry Express

Intel trading, anomalies, or ordinary items… Whatever the demand, we're happy to fulfill it.
