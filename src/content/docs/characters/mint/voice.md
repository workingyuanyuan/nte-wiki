Hotori Senpai? I… I don't know anything! Waah… It's not that I'm disloyal, I just don't want to be thrown out of Eibon by the ear next time… The hardest things in this world are passing exams and lying to her! She sees through everything!

### About Nanally

Nanally took on a super tough commission, sneaking into an extremely dangerous Anomalous Realm with Sakiri and Edgar! She said it was a heart-stopping, perilous adventure! Thankfully, her Colucci Ultimate Death Shadow Nether Punch saved the day! Haven't seen her for a while after that. Heard Hotori senpai grounded her… Ah, it's hard for geniuses to be recognized in this world!

### About Adler

Mr. Adler? Reliable and reassuring! Getting his help feels like a colleague handing you a brand-new pencil just when you realize you forgot yours in an exam. What's that scent called again? Mr. Adler told me before… Oh yeah, cedar!

### About Lacrimosa

Guess who's Strawberry Milk? Hehe, bet you can't tell—it's our one-and-only Lacrimosa Senpai! Surprised? Why isn't she tomato-flavored? Well, no reason. And don't you think a dreamy senpai goes well with strawberry milk?

### About Eibon Antique Shop

Eibon's a lively bunch, but it's one big cozy family at heart! I'm sure you'll find Eibon to be an awesome home!

### About ETD

"Each person an army." Isn't that thrilling? ETD is not just for eccentrics. They're cool, quirky people, but that doesn't mean that they can't be nice!

### About Bureau of Anomaly Control

Chatty downtime with colleagues is the best! We deal with super important things at the Bureau of Anomaly Control, but we're all human—fretting over mischievous kids or groaning about bills. But it's the little joys, like an extra day off or surprise milk tea from the group leader, that make our day!

### About CSU

Starting in the Containment Unit was a rollercoaster. I got in trouble a lot, but the veterans were there for me, laughing at my tears but staying late to help me. I'm aiming to be the reliable and cheerful heart of the team, just as solid as any ETD Elite!
