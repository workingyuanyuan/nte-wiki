## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair, according to Game8 and Prydwen |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan. How Boards work: [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Skills

Fadia redirects a portion of damage taken by teammates to herself while above 1 HP, sharing incoming damage if reduced to 1 HP. Her Skill links an enemy to share damage she takes, and losing enough HP charges and empowers her Ultimate.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Wordless Rejection, All Eyes on Me, Falling Contempt, Critical Riposte: Evasion Trigger | Wordless Rejection strikes up to five times, Falling Contempt plunges from midair, and Critical Riposte: Evasion Trigger counters after a Critical Dodge to reduce Break. All Eyes on Me redirects teammate damage to Fadia above 1 HP and shares damage taken at 1 HP. |
| Skill | Existence | Deals area Psyche DMG and inflicts Destructive Experience on the enemy with the highest Max HP in range. While active, that target takes a share of the damage Fadia receives until the effect expires, its Redirection DMG cap is reached, or both leave combat. |
| Ultimate | Agony to Euphoria | Enters Lilith state, dealing six Mental DMG hits and restoring HP; recasting Wordless Rejection performs up to five Mental DMG follow-up attacks. Losing HP to the Destructive Threshold refills its energy and boosts its damage and healing until Lilith state ends or another action interrupts it. |
| Support Skill | Outsider | Swings her cross shield to deal one instance of Psyche DMG to an area. |
| Passive 1 | Furnace of Guilt | Provides a Nova Enhancement that lets all allies drain Max HP from the target when Nova ends, up to a cap based on Fadia's base Max HP, restoring both parties' stats upon leaving combat. |
| Passive 2 | Denial and Plunder | Increases the team's Max HP while Fadia is in the team. |

## Awakenings

Each duplicate unlocks one Awakening of the player's choice; Resonances unlock at 3 and 6 Awakenings.

| Awakening | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Apostate | Fadia's damage redirect ratio is increased to 75%. |
| Instinct | Increases the damage ratio enemies take through Destructive Experience to 600% and raises its damage cap to 25% of Fadia’s Max HP. Deals immediate damage equal to the difference between the damage already taken and the damage cap when Destructive Experience is removed. |
| Curser of Blessings | Upon entering combat, Fadia increases her base Max HP by 30%. After leaving combat, she loses the increased Max HP. |
| Breaker of Taboos | Increases Fadia's healing ratio to 30% of Max HP while in Lilith form. Further increases the healing ratio to 60% of Max HP when her lost HP is greater than or equal to the Destructive Threshold. |
| Survivor of Death | Increases Fadia's CRIT Rate by 50% while in Lilith state. |
| Bringer of Love and Death | Inflicts Destructive Experience on up to 10 targets within range. Each Destructive Experience's duration and damage cap are calculated individually. |
| Resonance 3: The Disrespecting Saint | Increases the skill level of Wordless Rejection, Existence, and Agony to Euphoria by 1. |
| Resonance 6: The Unified Saint | Grants all team members 10% ATK while Fadia is on the team. |
