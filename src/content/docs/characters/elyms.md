## Skills

Elyms upgrades Connection Strength from Weak to Strong Connection using Basic Attack combos, Redirect Skills, and her Support Skill to scale her Ultimate and off-field Remembrance Follow-up Attacks while buffing team ATK and Follow-up Attack DMG.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Instant Glitch, Crosstalk, Interference, Memory Anchor, Falling Dream, Okay! Shake on It! | Instant Glitch hits up to five times at Weak Connection to unlock Intermittent Existence, or holds for Interference. Crosstalk hits up to three times at Medium/Strong Connection to unlock Goodbye to You. Aim casts Memory Anchor, Critical Dodge triggers Okay! Shake on It!, and Falling Dream plunges midair. |
| Skill | Counting Electric Sheep, The Ones Who Left, Intermittent Existence, Goodbye to You | The Ones Who Left deals area Cosmos DMG and unlocks Intermittent Existence at Weak Connection, raising Connection Strength to Medium, granting Optical Illusion, and scanning targets for Ultimate form. After Crosstalk, Goodbye to You consumes Optical Illusion stacks for scaled DMG, advances to Strong Connection, and rescans. |
| Ultimate | Do You Get Deja Vu | Auto-switches between single-target and AoE forms via scans (AoE at Weak Connection), scaling DMG Ratio by Connection Strength before resetting to Weak Connection. Grants team ATK and Follow-up Attack DMG buffs plus On the Line, triggering off-field Remembrance coordinated Follow-up Attack DMG until uses expire. |
| Support Skill | I'm Here | Deals Cosmos DMG, upgrades Weak Connection to Medium Connection when active, grants Optical Illusion, and scans nearby targets to switch Do You Get Deja Vu between single-target and AoE forms. |
| Passive 1 | Relay Node | Charge Enhancement stacks team Cosmos DMG when the active character gains Ultimate Energy, up to eight stacks. Remora Enhancement reapplies Remora to the same target after a short delay when an ally applies Remora, without chaining into itself. |
| Passive 2 | Pity System | Increases Elyms's Crit DMG. |
