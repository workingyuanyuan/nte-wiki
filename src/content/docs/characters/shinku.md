## Skills

Shinku builds Defiant Spirit with Basic Attacks, High-Speed Breach, and Breaching Tactics, then consumes it via Crimson Fury to enter Surging Crimson. Inside this state, she generates Fading Reason to cast Scarlet Descent and chains into Crimson Judgment and Dragonflame Verdict.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Special Combat Arts, Arrowfall, Special Combat Arts (Altered), Critical Riposte: Split-Second Victory, Tidal Assault, Hammerfall, Tidal Assault (Scorn), Critical Riposte: No Respite | Deals up to five Cosmos attacks, plunges, hold attacks, and Critical Ripostes to build Defiant Spirit. During Surging Crimson, Tidal Assault, Hammerfall, Tidal Assault (Scorn), and Critical Riposte: No Respite replace these moves and grant Fading Reason instead, with extra damage under Menacing Gaze. |
| Skill | High-Speed Breach, Scarlet Descent | High-Speed Breach charges toward the target for multiple Cosmos hits and gains Defiant Spirit. During Surging Crimson, it becomes Scarlet Descent, which consumes all Fading Reason to leap above the target and unleash a downward claw strike up to five times per state. |
| Ultimate | Crimson Fury, Crimson Judgment, Dragonflame Verdict | Consumes all Defiant Spirit to enter Surging Crimson, boosting DMG, interruption resistance, and movement while replacing normal skills. As the state nears its end, cast Crimson Judgment up to three times based on Scarlet Descent uses, then press again for Dragonflame Verdict. |
| Support Skill | Breaching Tactics | Delivers a forceful knee strike, dealing one instance of Cosmos DMG and gaining Defiant Spirit. |
| Passive 1 | Lone Stride | Applies Charge Enhancement, granting off-field allies matching Charge when the active character gains Ultimate Energy. While Shinku is active in Charge state, each trigger deals area DMG and stacks an ATK buff up to ten times. |
| Passive 2 | Wrathscale | Increases Ultimate DMG dealt against non-Boss enemies. |
