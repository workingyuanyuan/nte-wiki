## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan.

## Skills

Jiuyuan generates Rose Pact Bullets and binds enemies with Lethal Rose Pact through attacks and Vita Pistils, spending bullets for auto-targeted fire. Her Ultimate triggers Final Reckoning to execute Pact Settlement on all bound targets simultaneously, while expanding Vita Bud capacity.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | When Secrets Take Shape, Bird's Eye View, Info-Guided Arts, Opportunity Lock-On, Turning the Tide | Up to five Anima attacks grant Rose Pact Bullets and apply Lethal Rose Pact on the final hit, alongside plunge and Aim shots. Holding Basic Attack spends Rose Pact Bullets to auto-fire, prioritizing bound targets. After Critical Dodge, Turning the Tide binds, reduces Break, and grants bullets. |
| Skill | Intel Hunter | Dashes across the battlefield, pulling targets into control range and dealing multiple instances of Anima DMG. Grants Rose Pact Bullets and establishes Lethal Rose Pact on the targets. |
| Ultimate | Final Reckoning | Dashes at high speed while firing on surrounding targets before ending with a large Anima explosion. Triggers Final Reckoning, putting all nearby Lethal Rose Pact targets into a Pact Settlement stage and executing every settlement simultaneously on the final hit. |
| Support Skill | Intel Lock-On | Fires a curved bullet that deals one instance of Anima DMG and grants Rose Pact Bullets on hit. |
| Passive 1 | Seize the Moment | Blossom Enhancement spawns one additional Vita Bud and raises the on-field Vita Bud limit to six. |
| Passive 2 | Whispers at My Command | When Vita Pistils hit enemies, binds unbound targets with Lethal Rose Pact and triggers an additional settlement dealing extra Anima DMG on targets already bound. |

## Awakenings

Each duplicate unlocks one Awakening of the player's choice; Resonances unlock at 3 and 6 Awakenings.

| Awakening | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| To Know, To Balance | Increases Jiuyuan's ATK by 5% for each target on the field bound by Lethal Rose Pact, up to 15%. |
| Intel Turns Into Blades | Increases Lethal Rose Pact DMG Ratio by 100%. Restores HP to the entire team equal to 5% of the total damage accumulated by that Rose Pact whenever Pact Settlement is triggered by casting Final Reckoning. |
| Advantage Established | Deducts an additional 4 Break from the settlement target when Pact Settlement is triggered by casting Final Reckoning. |
| Strike to Kill | Increases DMG Ratio by 50% and affects targets within range when Pact Settlement is actively triggered. |
| Intel Superiority | Jiuyuan's DMG against targets bound by Lethal Rose Pact increases by 8%. |
| Know Every Secret | Deals 200% DMG Ratio to targets bound by Lethal Rose Pact each time they cast a skill. This effect has a 5s cooldown. |
| Resonance 3: Doubled Capacity | Increases the skill level of When Secrets Take Shape, Intel Hunter, and Final Reckoning by 1. |
| Resonance 6: Unhindered Passage | Reduces Anima Resistance of targets bound by Lethal Rose Pact by 10%. |
