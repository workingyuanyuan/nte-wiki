## Skills

Blackbird builds Malice and marks enemies with Forsaken Feather for off-field coordinated attacks, then casts Requies Animarum to enter Witch of Deliverance and spend Malice on enhanced attacks before ending with Flammae in Unum.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Fleeting Shadow, Roostcall, Lingering Affliction, Critical Riposte: Avian Instinct, Pyreflame, Bestowed Grace, Tidings Unto All, Searing Scar, Critical Riposte: Born of Calamity, Invocation | Fleeting Shadow, Roostcall, Lingering Affliction, and Avian Instinct deal Psyche DMG and build Malice via Invocation. In the Witch of Deliverance state, attacks shift to Pyreflame, Bestowed Grace, Tidings Unto All, Searing Scar, and Born of Calamity, which consume Malice for extra Malice DMG instead of generating it. |
| Skill | Netherplume Rite, Flames of Deliverance | Netherplume Rite deals Psyche DMG, gains Malice, and applies Forsaken Feather so allies trigger up to five coordinated Forsaken Bird area attacks. In Witch of Deliverance, Flames of Deliverance activates after Pyreflame's fifth hit or No Quarter, charging to deal Psyche DMG and consume Malice. |
| Ultimate | Requies Animarum, Flammae in Unum | Requies Animarum transforms Blackbird into the Witch of Deliverance with Hitstun Immunity and increased Movement Speed while boosting the team's ATK; its duration pauses off-field. Recasting triggers Flammae in Unum to exit the state and deal massive Psyche DMG. |
| Support Skill | Judgment Upon Me, Naught but Mercy | Judgment Upon Me transforms into Lonesome Bird to deal Psyche DMG and gain Malice. While in the Witch of Deliverance state, it becomes Naught but Mercy, dealing Psyche DMG and consuming Malice on hit. |
| Passive 1 | Thrushsong | Stain Enhancement boosts Stain's DMG Bonus and extends its duration. Nova Enhancement causes ending Nova to deal bonus Mental DMG scaling off actual damage taken while Nova was active, then reapplies Nova once without retriggering this reapplication. |
| Passive 2 | Ever Onward | Grants 20 Malice when Blackbird casts Judgment Upon Me. |

## Awakenings

Each duplicate unlocks one Awakening of the player's choice; Resonances unlock at 3 and 6 Awakenings.

| Awakening | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Renegade | Consumes all Malice after Witch of Deliverance casts Flammae in Unum. Increases the DMG Ratio of Flames of Deliverance by 150%. |
| Wayward Chronicle | Increases the DMG Ratio of Netherplume Rite by 100%. Increases DEF by 20% while in the Witch of Deliverance state. Restores HP equal to 400% of Base ATK after successfully casting Flames of Deliverance. |
| Blind Spot | Increases the DMG Ratio of Blackbird's attacks that consume Malice by 50%. |
| Bad Seed | Extends the duration of the ATK Bonus granted to all allies after casting Requies Animarum to 25s. Increases all allies' DMG dealt by 12% during this time. |
| Revenant | Reduces the target's Psyche, Lakshana, and Chaos Resistances by 8% for 25s after Blackbird hits them and deals DMG. |
| Mortal Coil | Increases the caster's Psyche DMG by 25% for 25s after casting Requies Animarum. Grants 4 Malice after Forsaken Bird performs a coordinated attack while Blackbird is not in the Witch of Deliverance state. |
| Resonance 3: Atonement Unveiled | Increases the skill level of Fleeting Shadow, Pyreflame, Netherplume Rite, Flames of Deliverance, Requies Animarum, and Flammae in Unum by 1. Increases Requies Animarum's DMG Ratio by 100% and Flammae in Unum's DMG Ratio by 50%. Grants 20 Malice after casting Requies Animarum. |
| Resonance 6: When the Stars Mourn | Increases the entire team's Crit DMG by 30% while Blackbird is on the team. |

## Mechanics in detail

- Malice is the gauge with a crow icon above Blackbird's HP bar, and it holds up to 60.[^1]
- Blackbird gains 20 Malice on entering combat, and Netherplume Rite gives 40, so in the first rotation one cast of Netherplume Rite fills the gauge.[^2]
- A full combo of normal-state Basic Attacks gives 20 Malice. Later rotations lack the Malice from entering combat, so filling the gauge takes Netherplume Rite and one full Basic Attack combo.[^3] With Passive 2 Ever Onward, casting Judgment Upon Me also gives 20 Malice.
- Holding Basic Attack during Flames of Deliverance casts Tidings Unto All, and tapping Basic Attack after it continues from Pyreflame's third hit.[^4]
- Netherplume Rite fills the Cycle gauge at once; this is what the game's "Instant Cycle" tag for Blackbird stands for.[^5]
- Forsaken Feather lasts 15 seconds; teammates attacking the marked target trigger Forsaken Bird attacks, at most once every 2 seconds and at most 5 times per mark.[^6]
- Flames of Deliverance lacks this instant fill of the Cycle gauge: it only deals damage.[^7]
- Flames of Deliverance spends 13 Malice for extra damage when enough is left. It has no cooldown but has to be activated again after every cast.[^8]
- Casting Flammae in Unum before Flames of Deliverance has dealt its damage cuts Flames of Deliverance short. Switching characters right after casting it does not: Blackbird stays on the field until it is finished.[^9]
- Requies Animarum raises the whole team's ATK by 20% for 15 seconds.[^10] Flammae in Unum does not grant this bonus.[^11]
- Witch of Deliverance lasts 16 seconds; the ring around the Ultimate icon shows the time left.[^12]
- While Blackbird is off the field, the Witch of Deliverance timer pauses but the 15-second ATK bonus keeps running.[^13]
- Blackbird is airborne when the Requies Animarum animation ends; tapping Basic Attack then starts from Pyreflame's third hit, which brings the first Flames of Deliverance about 2 seconds sooner.[^14]
- If Witch of Deliverance runs out before Flammae in Unum is cast, Blackbird changes back without dealing its damage.[^15]
- Thrushsong raises Stain's base DMG Bonus to 30% and its duration to 20 seconds.[^16]
- With Passive 1 Thrushsong, the extra Mental damage when Nova ends is 20% of the damage the target took during that Nova, and Nova is applied again; the Nova it adds does not trigger the passive again.[^17]
- With Renegade, Flammae in Unum spends all remaining Malice at once; with a full gauge, that part alone comes to a DMG ratio of about 3,360%.[^18]
- The 100% of Wayward Chronicle is a separate multiplier, so Netherplume Rite's damage doubles; Forsaken Bird's coordinated attacks do not gain it.[^19]
- The 50% of Blind Spot is a separate multiplier as well.[^20]
- The RES reduction of Revenant does not stack; later hits refresh its duration.[^21]
- Both Requies Animarum and Flammae in Unum trigger the 15-second Crit DMG bonus of "Crime and Punishment".[^22] Its wearer gains the team's bonus on top of her own: 16% and 32%, 48% in all, at rank 1.[^23]
