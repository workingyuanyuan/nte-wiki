## Profile

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class | S-Class, standard |
| Attribute | Chaos |
| Arc type | Liquid |
| Joined | Version 1.0 |

The official profile describes Daffodill of the Eibon antique shop as someone who never quite knows how to start a conversation and would rather observe anomalies than people; her blade serves only those she deems worthy.

The official notes and profile spell the name Daffodill. Prydwen and Game8 spell it Daffodil.

## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair, according to Game8 and Prydwen |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan. How Boards work: [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Skills

Daffodill focuses on Break Damage and quick-swapping: ally Support Skills stack Resonance to transform it into Echoes, her Ultimate inflicts Insight and unlocks Phantom Step charges, and her Discord Enhancement reduces enemy Break caps.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Still Waters, Light Prism, Mirror on Water, Displaced Edge, Crosshair Lock, Critical Riposte: Close Call | Still Waters performs up to five Chaos DMG strikes, while Light Prism boosts Break Damage contribution. Holding triggers Mirror on Water's whirlwind dash and marks; dodging mid-combo triggers invincible Displaced Edge without interrupting Still Waters. Crosshair Lock plunges midair, and Critical Dodge enables Critical Riposte: Close Call, reducing Break. |
| Skill | Resonance, Echoes | Resonance damages the target and gains Resonance stacks when any teammate casts a Support Skill, boosting Resonance's DMG and Break up to two stacks. At max stacks, Resonance is enhanced and replaced by Echoes, shifting stance to swing her blade with a rope for Chaos DMG. |
| Ultimate | Finale | Deals area Chaos DMG, unseals her left eye to increase damage, and inflicts Insight on nearby enemies. Also unlocks Phantom Step for up to two uses and resets Phantom Step charges upon casting. |
| Support Skill | Crossed Blades | Swings her blade in a slashing motion to deal one instance of Chaos DMG to the target. |
| Passive 1 | Blade Draw | Discord Enhancement reduces the target's Break cap while affected by Discord, stacking up to a cap of its initial Break cap and restoring it upon leaving combat. |
| Passive 2 | Cicada Shell | Increases the damage dealt by Phantom Step. |
