## Daily

### First Meet

Hi there! My name is Oneiroi. It's hard to pronounce, so just call me Iroi! Let me know if you ever need anything. Cya!

### Chat: Kicking Pebbles

I can't help but feel like kicking pebbles whenever I see them… I kick it further and further, until it disappears into somewhere I can't see. Do you do that too?

### Chat: School

My friends always have interesting things to say about school. I was home-schooled and didn't have any classmates. It's a shame that I never had a real school life.

### Chat: Evolutionary Theory

Natural selection is the survival of the fittest… That's the only thing my teacher said about the theory of evolution. She didn't have to explain, since that was the first dream "I" ever had…

### Sunny

The sun is shining, the flowers blooming…

### Rainy

My tail got soaked…

### Cold

I can't feel my limbs anymore.

### Hot

I want to get out of this scorching place.

### Morning Greeting

(Yawn) Good morning!

### Noon Greeting

It's time for a nap.

### Evening Greeting

There goes Mr. Sun.

### Good Night

The night. Has come.

### Hobbies

What do I like? I like anything other people like! Wait, you're saying some people like the things other people hate? Then they can fight each other and I'll side with the winner!

### Worries

My kiddo friends, Morpheus, Icelos, and Phantasos, always get themselves into trouble. I wonder if there's a way to get them under control…

### Topic: Sheep

I heard some cultures think goats are evil… Kiddos, do you think you're baddies? Oh, you don't? Kiddos, lying only makes our image worse!

### Topic: Room

When I was a kid, I spent a lot of time in the hospital because I got sick a lot. Back then, I thought everyone slept in white beds with those beeping and ticking machines. When I told colleagues at the charity house about this, they thought I slept in a haunted room!

### Topic: Dreamscapes

Do you like dreaming? Wanna take a look at my dreams? Just make sure you go through the gate with the bull horns. If you walk through the ivory gate, I won't be able to get you out. You'd have to watch yourself grow from an embryo back into yourself!

### About Myself: Family

My parents love me very much and have done a lot for me. They just want me to be happy. I'm grateful for them, yet I sometimes can't help but think…  if only a healthier kid was born instead…

### About Myself: Sleep

I guess I've always been sleeping. Even when I'm live and kicking, I'm still dream-walking!

### About Myself: Esper Ability

The shell known as Oneiroi gained a new life after her Esper ability awakened. That's not the real me. Do you get it? The real me is the one who's dreaming. The one talking to you is but a counterfeit role-playing a good kid!

### About? Myself?

My body was deteriorating, but my powers allowed me to live. She is not me. She lives in place of me. Am I still alive? I… don't know.

### About Mint

Mint senpai has limitless energy and passion. I want to be like her too! I'm lying? No, no, no, why would you say that? I mean it!

### About Shinku I

After I took Shinku into my dreams, she started hating on me. Did she not like what I showed her? Or… did she figure something out?

### About Shinku II

We're on good terms now. That skeptical dragon misunderstood me. At least according to Mint, Shinku and I "take good care" of each other now!

### About Nanally

Nanally is a little younger than I am, right? I'm a bit jealous of her, because she already knows what she wants to do at her age!

### Character Ascension I

I'll keep trying!

### Character Ascension II

Hmm… how much further can I go?

### Character Ascension III

This should be as far as I level up… Thanks for helping me.
