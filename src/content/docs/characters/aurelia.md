## Skills

Aurelia fights by entering the Cadenza state with Cadenza Aria and riding a jellyfish in Staccato to trigger periodic jellyfish blasts, building Crescendo ATK stacks while grouping enemies with Canzon Chorus and dealing bonus Psyche DMG after Nova ends.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Cappella, Legato, Staccato, Portamento, Critical Riposte: Syncopation | Legato performs up to four Psyche attacks. Staccato rides a jellyfish by holding Dodge while moving, firing periodic jellyfish blasts until stamina runs low, with its Critical Riposte firing four blasts. Portamento plunges from mid-air, and Critical Riposte: Syncopation counters after a Critical Dodge to reduce Break. |
| Skill | Cadenza Aria | Deals one instance of area Psyche DMG and enters the Cadenza state. |
| Ultimate | Canzon Chorus | Gathers nearby jellyfish, dealing six instances of area Psyche DMG and slightly pulling targets together. |
| Support Skill | Dissonance | Commands the jellyfish to strike, dealing one instance of area Psyche DMG on hit. |
| Passive 1 | Harmonics | Nova Enhancement: Deals three instances of Psyche DMG to the target after their Nova status ends. |
| Passive 2 | Crescendo | Increases Aurelia's ATK when a jellyfish blast hits a target, stacking up to ten times. |
