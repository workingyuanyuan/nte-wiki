## Daily

### First Meet

Newcomer? I—no, ahem, I mean, I, Nanally Coluccis, am the Ichi-daime of the future greatest family in all of Hethereau—the Colucci family! I'll have your back from now on.

### Chat: Grand Plan

If you've got nothing to …do, want to hear my grand plan for the family?

### Chat: Keenness

Argh, I'm so bored… Hmm?! Something doesn't feel quite right. I should probably scram…

### Chat: Books

Family Management… huh… Prologue… first of all… Ugh!!!

### Sunny

Clear skies! …Why are you looking at me like a pleased mother?

### Rainy

Boy, the rain is crazy… Alright, let's surf our way back!

### Cold

It's so cold! Hurry up. I'm turning into a popsicle!

### Hot

I feel like a fish on the beach right now. If only I had a Blizzi ice cream…

### Morning Greeting

Good morning! Any new commissions? The Coluccis are always good to go!

### Noon Greeting

Lunchtime! Is my favorite cheesy eggroll on the menu today?

### Evening Greeting

It's still early. Let's go out for another round.

### Good Night

Huh? Is it this late already? One more game and I'm going to sleep!

### Hobbies

Snacks, manga, gaming, bug catching, parkour, beating up minions, and… argh, I've lost count.

### Worries

Worry? Hmm… Uh… I know! Someone ate the pudding I left in the fridge. I was totally looking forward to enjoying it while reading the latest issue of "Monthly Fantasy".

### Topic: Family

A boss who doesn't stand by their family is not a real boss… We owe everything to the family business for who we are today. Betraying the family is betraying yourself! …How's that? Cool, right? I've always wanted to say these lines from "Sin City Chronicles"!

### Topic: Electromagnetic Attraction

First a crackle-POP, then a snap-ZAP, and bam—I'm upside down and clinging to the wall! Oh, oh! And when I go whoosh and drop in right in front of the bad guys? That's my signature entrance!

### Topic: Hair Care

First, rinse the fur with clean water. Then use the special cleanser, blow-dry with warm air until it's fluffy-but-damp, spritz on some conditioners, switch to cool air to finish drying, and trim off any split ends. That's how Adler taught me—after all, the Ichi-daime has to look the part!

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

To get back the family heirloom, I leapt onto the outer pipes of the building! But then—get this—some creep set up a broken live cable just waiting for me. I felt this ZZZZZT jolt and then I was falling! But I swear someone grabbed me! Next thing I know, my body's all spark-crackle, and we're just standing there—on the wall! As for the price… hmm… didn't feel anything weird. Maybe the cost was too small to notice? Is this what they call… plot armor?

### About Myself: Wounds

This wound's probably from an anomaly. This one is from bumping into a fire hydrant when I chased Danzaburou after it stole food from a grandma… Ah, and this is from teaching those robbers a good lesson. All minor injuries, doesn't hurt one bit! …Huh? Holes on my clothes?! Ahhh… guess I'm in trouble with the old man…

### About Myself: Street Fighter

Not trying to brag or anything, but before I joined Eibon, everyone on Bluebeard Road and Torchwood Avenue knew who I was! I didn't even need to fight them with my Esper Ability… Uh, what? Did you just say you wanted to learn more about me? Thanks but no thanks. I don't want no trouble around Eibon!

### About Hotori

A mysterious antique shop owner with an enigmatic background sounds so cool! I really want to be like her. Should I start by learning to drink?

### About Adler

There is nothing old man can't do, can you believe it? I've tried to dig for secrets a few times when Boss was drunk, and all I got was all sorts of rumors… except Sakiri said none of those were true. Boy, is the guy some sort of trade secret?

### About Sakiri

It seems like Sakiri… keeps a whole lot of things to herself. But as the leader, I'm very tolerant of my family members. Sakiri can continue being herself. I mean, she might just open up to me someday when I'm as powerful as Master Daffodill!

### About Edgar

There seems to be a computer in Edgar's head. He can even answer bizarre questions like "Why are round pizzas put in square boxes and then cut into triangles?" or "Why is there more air than chips in a bag of chips?" Honestly, they should have designed Edgar's head the way they have Taygedo's.

### About Daffodill

I've never seen anyone with a more regular routine than Master Daffodill! She never slacks off! I mean, she even brushes her teeth and eats with her back straight! And when she trains, she never takes a break… Uh, I just happened to see it while passing by. I wasn't peeking! I swear!

### About Taygedo

Taygedo? He's still on probation, but if he does as I say, I just might consider giving him an important role in the family down the road. He really needs to consider himself lucky working for me!

### About Eibon Antique Shop

A mysterious shop in a quiet alley, an enigmatic shop owner, a high-combat-value cold bodyguard, a versatile robot-like butler, and… a mascot! It looks like a setting straight out of a hot-blooded manga! I mean, life at Eibon turned out different than I'd imagined… but I can already smell the plot brewing! People all over Hethereau are going to know Eibon and the Coluccis. No, people all over the world are going to know Eibon and the Coluccis!
