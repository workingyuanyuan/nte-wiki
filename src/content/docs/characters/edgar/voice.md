## Daily

### First Meet

Greetings, I'm Edgar, staff member of the Eibon Antique Shop. When there are commissions, I also work with others as an apprentice anomaly hunter.

### Chat: Scarf

My mother hand-knitted this scarf. It's light and warm. What do I wear when it gets hot? I switch to a silk one I got from my father. It has the same pattern.

### Chat: Suitcase

I wish it could be lighter. Is this what they call the weight of knowledge?

### Chat: Anomaly

I like anomalies. I joined Eibon because I wanted to learn more about them. First, I can observe Taygedo up close…

### Sunny

Let's move the plant to the yard for some sunlight.

### Rainy

I just saw Taygedo run out without an umbrella. Is he waterproof…?

### Cold

I won't get cold with my scarf.

### Hot

I wanna live in a fridge.

### Morning Greeting

Morning. What are your plans for today? Can I tag along?

### Noon Greeting

Taygedo likes to take siestas. He's probably tucked up cozy somewhere.

### Evening Greeting

Another productive day. I learned a lot.

### Good Night

When I was little, my mother told me the story of the Cloud Sheep. When a child falls asleep, Cloud Sheep fluff up the pillow to make them sleep through the night. However, Cloud Sheep sleep early, so if you stay up too late, they might already be sleeping on the moon. I hope you meet them tonight though. Good night, Appraiser.

### Hobbies

Reading, and observing anomalies.

### Worries

I've been having troubles with my eyesight. Nanally said if I keep reading in dim lights, I'll end up with glasses like her…

### Topic: Frizzle

By the way, are Frizzles the favorite anomaly of seagulls?

### Topic: Nacupeda

Does Nacupeda have wishes like "I want to eat lots and lots of coins today"?

### Topic: Bonzita

Always forgetting, always curious…? Hmm…

### Topic: Danzaburou

Danzaburou can shapeshift into human form to steal things. Their disguise isn't difficult to see through though: even when they transform into humans, they still wear their signature red napkins. When you're shopkeeping, pay extra attention to customers like that.

### Topic: Officer Whisker

I heard the Whiskers are on good terms with an anomaly called the "Justice Executioner," which looks like a jungle of traffic lights. The Whiskers are even using the anomaly as a base. How do they even train and rest there? I hope I could see for myself… Hmm, actually, almost no one has ever seen what the Justice Executioner looks like. Could it be an urban legend…?

### About Myself: Esper Ability

It's not some extraordinary ability, but Hotori said "Don't get poached by those delivery guys." Nanally also told me "The Colucci family can't do without you!" Well, I'm just happy to help.

### About Adler

I want to be as organized and meticulous as Adler. I still have so much to learn.

### About Hotori

Hotori always says that salvaging antiques is about having a good eye. But what does that mean? No books explain this very well… Or is that just the experience of looking into antiques?

### About Sakiri

Kiroumaru is rather heavy… Sakiri must be very strong.

### About Kiroumaru

Actually, I've been wanting to peek into Kiroumaru's stomach. Kiroumaru agreed, but Sakiri doesn't want me to do it…

### About Nanally

The boss once said that we should hold a meeting to discuss a book called "Family Management." I've already prepared some notes, but she never has time…

### About Daffodill

When I'm with Daffodill, even if we don't talk much, it's never awkward. Maybe… we both enjoy the peace and quiet?

### About Taygedo

I decided to apply after I saw Taygedo's hand-drawn recruiting flyer. Taygedo was my first friend in Hethereau.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

It's like a second home. Everyone is close like family.
