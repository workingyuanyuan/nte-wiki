## Profile

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Class | S-Class, standard |
| Attribute | Lakshana |
| Arc type | Plasma |
| Joined | Version 1.0 |

The official profile describes Hathor as an elite courier, a job her Mentor gave her: precise, punctual, and able to find an unmarked drop-off in a storm without a map.

## How to obtain

| Way | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Board | Standard S-Class characters are on the permanent Standard Board of Scarborough Fair, according to Game8 and Prydwen |
| Selection box, Version 1.2 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box by mail to every player who created a character before 2026-08-19 05:59 (UTC+8) |
| Selection box, Version 1.4 | S-Class Standard Character Selection Box from the City Tycoon "Puka Membership Program" |

The box gives one of six characters of the player's choice: Sakiri, Baicang, Hathor, Fadia, Daffodill or Jiuyuan. How Boards work: [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Skills

Hathor builds Express Delivery Power stacks using her Skill, Critical Riposte: Zero-Error Operation, and Efficiency Boost passive. Casting her Ultimate enters Emergency Delivery state, granting an ATK bonus from those stacks and upgrading Aerial Command into Cyclone Strike, which consumes stacks for rapid combos.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Rapid Delivery, Final Dispatch, To The Skies, Critical Riposte: Zero-Error Operation | Rapid Delivery executes up to five Lakshana strikes, Final Dispatch plunges from midair, and holding Basic Attack performs To The Skies to knock up and plunge foes. Critical Riposte: Zero-Error Operation counters after a Critical Dodge, dealing area Lakshana DMG, reducing Break, and granting Express Delivery Power. |
| Skill | Aerial Command, Cyclone Strike | Aerial Command slashes enemies ahead to gain two Express Delivery Power stacks, or can be held to charge, expanding its Lakshana AoE and applying Five-Star Tracking so enemy hits on allies grant three stacks. During Emergency Delivery, it becomes Cyclone Strike, consuming stacks for a rapid motorcycle combo. |
| Ultimate | Rider Express | Summons a thorn motorcycle to circle and dive-bomb enemies for area Lakshana DMG, entering Emergency Delivery state. Stops gaining Express Delivery Power, upgrades Aerial Command to Cyclone Strike, increases Movement Speed, and grants an ATK bonus from stored stacks. Switching characters ends Emergency Delivery immediately. |
| Support Skill | Impact Point Designation | Strikes from the air and kicks the target to trigger an impact, dealing two instances of area Lakshana DMG. |
| Passive 1 | Delay Warning | Provides a Remora Enhancement that extends the target's Remora duration and increases Crit Rate when attacking targets affected by Remora. |
| Passive 2 | Efficiency Boost | Grants one stack of Express Delivery Power after defeating a target. |
