## Skills

Lacrimosa switches between Tomato Metal and Tomato Percussion forms to stack Nightmare and change Working Day Judgement's attack pattern. Through Devilish Inspiration, she can also learn enemy techniques to temporarily replace Morning Tomato with Devilish Gift.

| Slot | Skills | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Attack | Sweet and Sour, Tomato Metal, Tomato Percussion, Flavor Variation, Devilish Inspiration, Lycopersicum Meteors, fff Rest, Nightmare | Switches via Flavor Variation between five-hit Tomato Metal and Tomato Percussion Chaos combos, determining Working Day Judgement's form. Includes plunging Lycopersicum Meteors, Critical Dodge counter fff Rest, and Aim-mode Devilish Inspiration to learn enemy techniques replacing Morning Tomato. Hits stack Nightmare up to ten times. |
| Skill | Morning Tomato, Devilish Gift | Morning Tomato throws constructs dealing AoE Chaos DMG, inflicting Nightmare, and chaining into the fifth Tomato Metal or Tomato Percussion hit. When replaced via Devilish Inspiration, Redirect Skill Devilish Gift copies and casts the learned enemy skill while granting a portion of its owner's ATK. |
| Ultimate | Working Day Judgement | Adapts to the active Basic Attack style, either controlling a Truck for ten Chaos hits in Tomato Metal or dismantling a Car for five Chaos hits in Tomato Percussion, inflicting Nightmare on all targets in range. |
| Support Skill | Microwake | Manipulates Esper Ability Constructs to deal one instance of Chaos DMG in an area. |
| Passive 1 | Tomato Banquet | Discord Enhancement deals additional ATK-based DMG when Discord triggers against a target that is already Broken. |
| Passive 2 | Sleep In | Grants one extra cast of Morning Tomato after using Tomato Metal or Tomato Percussion five times during the skill cooldown, activating once per cooldown. |
