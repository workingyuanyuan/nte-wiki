## Daily

### First Meet

Welcome to Eibon. I'm Alois V. Adler, the butler. Please, call me Adler. If you need anything, let me know. I'm at your service.

### Chat: Business

This month's revenue… is not looking too promising.

### Chat: Lists

Booze, candy, manga, game cartridges, anti-rust oil… yup, that's everything.

### Chat: Sunya

Thank you all for the help today.

### Sunny

It's perfect weather for an outing. I should prepare some bento boxes for Nanally and the others.

### Rainy

It seems weather forecasts aren't always reliable. Let's find somewhere to wait out the rain.

### Cold

I should remind everyone to dress more warmly, and I'll prepare some cold medicine just in case.

### Hot

One shouldn't indulge in too much cooling off in hot weather—stomach aches are rather unpleasant, after all.

### Morning Greeting

Good morning. How's your rest? Come for breakfast when you're ready.

### Noon Greeting

Anything you'd like to eat? I'll get cooking.

### Evening Greeting

Hey, relax. It's our free time now, customers usually don't come at these hours.

### Good Night

Getting enough sleep is essential for maintaining good health… Of course, if you'd like to chat more, I'm always here.

### Hobbies

Cooking is a discipline—from selecting ingredients to receiving feedback, each meal is a journey. I love preparing meals for everyone at Eibon, though it would be better if Sakiri were less picky.

### Worries

Troubles? The shop's running at a deficit, my lady bought another counterfeit antique, and Nanally's schemes bring the neighbors to our doorstep. These things give me a headache, but I worry more about everyone neglecting their well-being. As Eibon's butler, I pay extra attention to such concerns.

### Topic: Anomalies

Most Oddities are actually quite friendly—they just don't know how to express themselves, or perhaps their ways are different from what we're used to. If you get the chance, try communicating with them. You might just discover a completely different world.

### Topic: Etiquette

Neat attire leaves a good impression, but true etiquette is about considering others' feelings and not imposing our habits. Forcing others to follow our principles is the greatest breach of etiquette.

### Topic: Tips

The secret to handling everything quickly? For routine matters, I simply prepare lists in advance and follow the plan. Sometimes unexpected situations do arise—that's when patience and a bit of luck come in handy… I suppose with enough experience, you start to recognize patterns in how to handle things.

### About Myself: Armor

This armor is called Enlightenment. It's not mine; I'm just fortunate enough to use it. Sunya resides within it, making household chores easier.

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

It would be difficult for me to have a truly neutral stance on Esper abilities or anomalies, as I undoubtedly have vested interests with them… Most people are puzzled when they first meet me, wondering how a blind man can perceive the world around him with such clarity. I was once just an ordinary blind person, dependent on others for many things. It was the Enlightenment that gifted me with this special ability to perceive the world, and with it, a duty to protect.

### About Myself: Glasses

These glasses? Nanally bought them for me with her first earnings from commissions after joining Eibon. She was absolutely beaming when she said, "This is how the gentleman thug dresses in movies!" Seeing her so excited, I couldn't help but accept this unique gesture. As for her own pair, she got them to embody more of a… "Ichi-daime boss" vibe? Such gifts are quite in style with Nanally.

### About Myself: Being Perfect

Perfect, huh? You're far too kind. I simply have a few more years behind me, having seen a bit more of the world. When I was their age, I dreamed of being as bright as they are now.

### About Hotori

My lady carries many burdens… I'm ashamed to say I can only help with mundane matters, but it's my honor to shoulder even a small portion of her responsibilities.

### About Nanally

Nanally is a simple kid. Thanks to her, Eibon has become much more lively. I wish when things happen, she would be more… But I'm not complaining about the noise. This is exactly the age when they should be playing around.

### About Sakiri

Sakiri isn't great at expressing her feelings directly. For someone as sensitive as her, actions speak louder than words. She often cares more than she shows. Giving her space helps her open up gradually.

### About Edgar

Being unsociable isn't necessarily a bad thing—everyone has their own way of living, and Edgar himself isn't troubled by his difficulty in communicating with others, which is perfectly fine. Besides, he has Sakiri and Nanally by his side who understand him best, and they'll always be his most attentive listeners.

### About Daffodill

Daffodill shows unwavering loyalty to my lady, handling all matters concerning her personally—she's someone we can truly rely on. Thanks to her support, I can focus on taking care of other tasks… Though sometimes, I wish she would take better care of herself too.

### About Taygedo

I'm grateful for Taygedo's regular help around the shop, especially those interesting little inventions of his. Though Sakiri, Nanally, and even my lady joke about selling him to settle debts, they can't help but ask about him when he hasn't been around for a while.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

Eibon wasn't always this lively. When we first settled on Hankaku Street, it was run-down. Sakiri barely spoke, and both my lady and Daffodill were busy. Things changed when we found Taygedo, and even more when Nanally and Edgar joined. The shop was filled with warmth and laughter. Now, with Appraiser here, Eibon feels like a true home.

### Character Ascension I

Learning knows no bounds.

### Character Ascension II

Could never stop improving.

### Character Ascension III

A moment worth remembering.

### Gift Received I

What a wonderful gift. Thank you, Appraiser.

### Gift Received II

I was just reminded of some things in the past… I like the gift a lot. Thanks.

### Gift Received III

Appraiser, I've always known you had great taste.

### Gift Received IV
