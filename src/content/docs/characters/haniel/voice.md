## Daily

Hey there! I'm Haniel Cresswell, but you can just call me Haniel. If you need anything, shout my name! I swear on the name of "E.A.R"—if it's your voice, I'll always hear it!

### Evening Greeting

### Good Night

Late night's the best time to catch up on anime. Don't worry, just one episode before bed! Probably…

### Hobbies

My goal? Collect every limited-edition collab headset! The new ones I can snag at launch, but for vintage ones, it's all about patience. Sure, even the best-kept old headsets can't beat cheap modern ones in sound quality—but classic OSTs deserve that analog crackle. I'll show you my collection sometime.

### Worries

Big worries? Not really. Small ones? Tough homework, noisy Illica, vague delivery addresses, picky customers… nothing a good meal and sleep can't fix. Haniel's always back at full power the next day!

### Topic: Anomalies

The elders said there was a time when humans, Espers, and anomalies were always at odds. But by the time I was born, anomalies felt more like family—pets, even. Sure, not everything's perfect, but most anomalies are super cute!

### Topic: Manners

Knives on the left, forks on the right, and no slurping your soup? If you can't enjoy food freely, what's the point? Like spicy sticks—put 'em on a gilded plate with violin music… still won't beat eating 'em straight from the bag.

### Topic: Fellow Enthusiasts

It's wild how people connect—just sharing love for a character can make two strangers close friends. There are things I can't even tell my real-life friends that I'll share with someone online in a heartbeat. I've got one really special online friend… I hope we meet offline someday.

### About Myself: Vest

The worst thing about following a series? Hiatus notices. You want to lock the author in a room till they finish! But then when it's my turn to write… heh. That's what alt accounts are for! I'm not skipping updates—I'm… looking for inspiration!

### About Myself: Esper Abilities

I used to envy Espers. Like the heroes in anime—chosen ones, saving the world. But when it was my turn, I realized: I don't want to be the protagonist. I want the power to protect others. If I'd been a little stronger back then… maybe it would've survived like the other kittens. But I'm not telling you this to make you sad. Let's go visit together sometime. It's a really pretty place.

### About Myself: Freedom

"Some birds are meant for cages—their feathers fluff up from fine feed and water. But some birds can't be caged. Their wings only shine in the open sky." Me? I want a warm home and freedom. Maybe that's greedy, but I'm happy with my life.

### About Jiuyuan

Working at Sterry Express doesn't get in the way of school, pays well, and comes with a world-saving mission! Jiuyuan's also super generous with bonuses—what's not to love? I, Haniel, will become Sterry's top courier! If only Jiuyuan wouldn't get so mad—stress causes wrinkles, y'know… cough.

### About Jenson

The first time I met Jenson, I thought—"Wow, this guy is so cool!" Like a dependable anime adult! Ask him anything, he's got an answer! No way he's just a normal shopkeep—bet he's hiding some secret identity. That's how it goes in shows!

### About Illica

Ugh, that noisy Silly! If I'd known we'd reunite at Sterry, I wouldn't have written so much in his yearbook… But hey, he wrote a bunch in mine too. Even gave me a headset I'd wanted for ages as a grad gift. So… I'll let it slide.

### About Hathor

I really wanna try Hathor's motorcycle. Must feel amazing to ride! People say she seems cold—but that's only 'cause they don't know her. With me around, even Esper Ability blowups are no big deal! Go Haniel! No fear!

### About Sterry Express

I saved Hathor when her Esper powers backfired. Jiuyuan brought me into Sterry after that. Then I met Jenson, and Illica showed up again… It's like the start of "The Haniel Chronicles"… no, wait, maybe "Courier X Stelry"? "Masked Courier"? We'll work on it.

### About Eibon Antique Shop

Hidden relic shop on a shady street vs. undercover express company in plain sight—this arc needs a movie adaptation!
