## Sea Angler

Sea Angler is the fishing hobby, one of the Hethereau Hobbies. It opens at City Tycoon level 3, from the menu: City Tycoon, Hethereau Hobbies, Sea Angler.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Cast | Pick a rod and a bait at a fishing spot. A fish bites after a few seconds |
| Reel in | Keep the cursor on the fish's bar to drain its stamina before the line runs out |
| Size | Each catch is graded B, A or S. Bigger fish sell for more and give more Fishing EXP; better rods make bigger fish more likely |
| Sell | The Fish Hold buys any fish at the normal price. The Fish Market buys three featured fish a day at 200% to 500% more |
| Pay | Selling pays Scale Coins, which buy bait and rods. It also spends City Stamina and gives 1,000 Fons per point spent |
| Level | Fishing levels pay Annulith. Fishing level 10 gives an outfit for [Esper Zero](/characters/zero/) |

Sources disagree on the City Stamina cost: GameWith gives 1 point per fish, while the NGA analysis says the cost differs by species. A Fish Market sale spends extra City Stamina in line with its higher price; fish still sell for Scale Coins when City Stamina runs out, without the Fons; and bait raises the odds of certain fish but cannot bring a fish the spot does not have.[^1] Fishing level 10 allows trading Scale Coins for Beetle Coins.[^2]

## Bagel

Bagel is the in-game social app, added in [Version 1.1](/versions/1-1/). Players post screenshots and videos and react to each other's posts. Reactions earn Praise Jam, spent in the Bagel shop.

Reacting to other players' posts pays up to 2,000 Praise Jam a day, without posting anything:

| Action | Praise Jam each | Counted per day | Daily total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View a post | 100 | 5 | 500 |
| Like a post | 200 | 3 | 600 |
| Comment on a post | 300 | 3 | 900 |

The game tallies these reactions once a day, so the Praise Jam shows up the following day.[^3]

[^3]: [GameWith, Bagel and Praise Jam guide](https://gamewith.net/nte/76018)

Your own posts earn Praise Jam on top of that. Each day the game scores your posts at 5 points per view, 30 per like and 60 per comment, follow or bookmark, then converts the score in steps with falling rates. 950 points give 400 Praise Jam, 5,716 give 1,199, 14,798 give 1,999, and the daily maximum is 13,998 at 1,128,000 points.[^4]
