## Types and classes

Arcs come in five types and three classes: S (gold), A (purple) and B (blue). Every Arc has ATK as its first stat, a second stat, and an effect with five ranks. Arcs level to 80 and ascend at levels 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 and 80. Limited S-Class Arcs come from Arc Research Programs; see [Boards and Arc Research](/systems/boards/).

## Effects

Values run from rank 1 to rank 5.

| Arc | Effect, rank 1 to rank 5 |
| --- | --- |
| Blow up the Crowd | Increases the active character's ATK by 10%–14% while the wearer is off-field, and increases the active character's ATK by 2%–3.2% when the wearer deals damage, up to 4 stacks and triggering at most once every 2s. Resets the effect when the wearer becomes the active character. Increases Psyche DMG by 12%–20% while the wearer is the active character, and increases Psyche DMG by 2%–3.2% when dealing Psyche DMG with a Basic Attack, up to 10 stacks. Triggers at most once every 0.3s. Resets the effect when switching characters. |
| Day Off | Increases wearer's ATK by 30%–50%. Unlocks Arc: Eclipse. Arc: Eclipse — The wearer creates a 40s Eclipse. During the Eclipse, each enemy defeated restores 6–10 Ultimate Energy, up to 5 times. (cooldown: 300s). |
| Echoes on Hold | Increases ATK by 15%–30%. After the Wearer casts their Ultimate, dealing Follow-up Attack DMG 3 times grants the whole team 12%–20% DEF Ignore and grants the Wearer an additional 10%–20% DEF Ignore for 20s. Resets the duration when triggered again. This effect is unique within the team. Grants the whole team 8%–20% Cosmos Resistance Penetration for 20s when the Wearer casts a Redirect Skill. Resets the duration when triggered again. This effect is unique within the team. Triggers all the above effects immediately when the Wearer enters combat. |
| Fluff of Fearlessness | Increases ATK by 25%–45% for 10s after the wearer casts an Ultimate. Effect does not stack. |
| Hethereau's Keeper | Increases ATK by 15%–25%. Increases damage dealt to Bosses by 15%–25%. Unlocks Arc: Officer Whisker. Arc: Officer Whisker — Summons Officer Whisker to assist in combat. Officer Whisker continuously attacks enemies, dealing 100%–200% of the wearer's ATK as damage per hit. Lasts 30s. Cooldown: 60s. |
| Marching Beyond Time | Increases ATK by 16%–32%. Enters Wastelab and clears current Wastetime when the wearer casts a Redirect Skill. While in Wastelab, allies gain 1 stack of Wastetime each time they use a Redirect or Support Skill, up to 3 stacks. When the wearer uses their Ultimate in Wastelab, they exit Wastelab and consume all Wastetime, increasing Ultimate Crit DMG by 24%–48%, plus an additional 8%–16% per Wastetime consumed. Consuming 3 Wastetime stacks at once grants an extra 12%–24% DEF Ignore for 70s. |
| Reality Refuge | Increases Anima DMG by 15%–25%. Increases the wearer's Attachment DMG by 10%–15%. Increases the Attachment DMG bonus to 20%–30% for 6s when the wearer casts an Ultimate. Effect does not stack. |
| The Rain That Shook the World | Deals 30%–42% increased Cosmos DMG with the wearer's Redirect Skill and Ultimate. Increases the wearer's Cycle Intensity by 36–72 for 15s after casting a Redirect Skill. Resets the duration when triggered again. |
| Your Happiness is Priceless | Increases HP by 24%–40%. Grants one of the following random effects when the wearer casts an Ultimate: 1. Recovers 20%–40% of Max HP for the team member with the lowest HP percentage. 2. Grants a Shield equal to 20%–40% of the wearer's Max HP for 15s. 3. Recovers 10%–20% HP for all team members. Triggers once every 30s. |
| Far Side of the Moon | Increases Crit Rate by 15%–27%. Increases the wearer's Redirect Skill DMG by 28%–56%. Increases Basic Attack DMG by 28%–56% and Lakshana DMG by 20%–40% for 15s after casting a Redirect Skill. |
| Fluff of Fleetness | Increases 5%–9% ATK every 1s while the wearer is the active character, up to 5 stacks. Resets when the wearer leaves the field. |
| The Last Rose | Increases ATK by 14%–28%. Grants 1 stack of Chaos Thorn each time the wearer deals DoT. Increases CRIT DMG by 6%–12% per stack for 3s. Triggers at most once every 0.3s, up to 10 stacks, and refreshes the duration when retriggered. Grants 10 stacks of Chaos Thorn immediately when the wearer casts a Redirect Skill. Extends the Broken state of a Broken enemy by 3s when the wearer deals damage to them (triggers at most once per Break Effect). |
| The Wrong Gate | Increases ATK by 16%–32%. When the wearer performs healing, increases the wearer's Anima DMG dealt by 30%–60% and all allies' DMG dealt by 15%–30% for 20s. Resets the duration when triggered again. Effects with the same name do not stack. |
| Youthful Fantasy | Increases Break Intensity by 60–88. Seals Arc: Black Tome with two chains by default. Unleashes 1 chain each time any character on the team casts a Support Skill. | Blushing Mirage | Increases ATK by 20%–32%. After casting Ultimate, increases the wearer's Cosmos DMG dealt by 32%–51.2% and allows the wearer to ignore 12%–19.2% of enemy DEF for 20s. Resets the duration when triggered again. |
