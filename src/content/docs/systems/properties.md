## Properties

Pegasus Residence (Version 1.1) and Stillmeadow House (Version 1.3) need City Tycoon level 13. Crops grown at Stillmeadow House can be sold for Fons by spending City Stamina.

| Property | Price (Fons) | Anomaly Furniture slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wiener Apartments | 200,000 | 7 |
| Eden Apartments | 1,000,000 | 9 |
| Skyview Halls | 1,400,000 | 9 |
| Golden Capital | 2,000,000 | 9 |
| Stillmeadow House | 6,900,000 | 8 |
| Pegasus Residence | 7,500,000 | 10 |
| Fenglin Villa | 8,000,000 | 11 |

| Coocoo Alert | Coocoo Alert clears your warrant when you enter the property. | All |
