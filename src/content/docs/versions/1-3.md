Each needs the main story quest "Prologue - A New Destination - Head to Eibon Antique Shop". First half: 2026-08-19, after maintenance, to 2026-09-09 05:59 (UTC+8). Second half: 2026-09-09, after maintenance, to 2026-09-30 05:59 (UTC+8).
