## Characters and Arcs

Each needs the main story quest "Prologue - A New Destination - Head to Eibon Antique Shop".

| Item | Kind | Where | Open (UTC+8) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Shinku](/characters/shinku/) | New limited S-Class character | "Before the Dawn" Limited Board | 2026-07-08, after maintenance, to 2026-07-29 05:59 |
| Blushing Mirage | New limited S-Class Arc | "Resolve Special" Arc Research Program, with Tri-Keys or Resolve Keys | 2026-07-08, after maintenance, to 2026-07-29 05:59 |
| [Iroi](/characters/iroi/) | New limited S-Class character | "The Lifeline" Limited Board | 2026-07-29, after maintenance, to 2026-08-19 05:59 |
| The Wrong Gate | New limited S-Class Arc | "Dreamgate Special" Arc Research Program, with Tri-Keys or Dreamgate Keys | 2026-07-29, after maintenance, to 2026-08-19 05:59 |

"Before the Dawn" also carried [Mint](/characters/mint/), [Edgar](/characters/edgar/) and [Adler](/characters/adler/); "The Lifeline" carried [Haniel](/characters/haniel/), [Aurelia](/characters/aurelia/) and [Skia](/characters/skia/).

| Content | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
