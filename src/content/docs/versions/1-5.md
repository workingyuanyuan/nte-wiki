## Characters and Arcs

All four are open from 2026-11-11, after maintenance, to 2026-12-02 05:59 (UTC+8). Each needs the main story quest "Prologue - A New Destination - Head to Eibon Antique Shop".

| Item | Kind | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Elyms](/characters/elyms/) | New limited S-Class character | "Android Dreams" Limited Board |
| Echoes on Hold | New limited S-Class Arc | "Echo Special" Arc Research Program, with Tri-Keys or Echo Keys |
| [Shinku](/characters/shinku/) | Limited S-Class character, rerun | "Before the Dawn" Limited Board |
| Blushing Mirage | Limited S-Class Arc, rerun | "Resolve Special" Arc Research Program, with Tri-Keys or Resolve Keys |

Both Boards also carry the A-Class characters [Mint](/characters/mint/), [Edgar](/characters/edgar/) and [Adler](/characters/adler/). Roll counts are kept per Board name: rolls on "Before the Dawn" count toward Resolve Perks only, and progress from the Board's earlier run carries over.

From 2026-12-02, the second half brings a new Limited Board with the S-Class character Exe ("Offline Equations" on Game8, with the Arc "Dotted Horizon" on Prydwen) and a rerun of [Chaos](/characters/chaos/) on "Forsaken Path".
