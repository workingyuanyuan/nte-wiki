## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | If the Ultimate is ready at the start, use it, then press the Skill as the red line crosses a peak. | [2:14](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=134) |
| Tip | Without the Ultimate, use the Skill three times to start its cooldown, then two Basic Attack strings; when the Skill is nearly back to 3 charges, tap it three times again, and repeat until the Ultimate is ready. | [2:19](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=139) |
| Tip | In the low floors of Beyond the Rails, Chiz can take boss stages alone; Haniel in the background for constant ATK is better still, since about 30% of Chiz's damage is direct and uses ATK. | [3:21](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=201) |
| Tip | Holding the Skill is generally not recommended: spending all Grain deals more fixed damage, but the following Skills lose much of theirs. | [4:11](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=251) |
| Tip | Ideally five Skills fit in the 7 s Ultimate, but peaks and troughs cannot be controlled, so to keep Grain from falling use three or four at most; fill with Basic Attacks while the Skill cools down or in a trough. | [4:23](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=263) |
| Reason | With weak early gear Chiz far outdamages everyone else, so filling a team lowers her value for the cost. | [5:08](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=308) |
| Tip | With few Awakenings, avoid holding the Skill: spent Grain costs the first Skills of the next Ultimate; with many Awakenings and fast income, a short spin after each Ultimate works, such as spending a few thousand Grain, soon earned back with the 10%-per-second interest Awakening. | [9:13](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=553) |
| Reason | Before Awakening 5, the first rotation relies on the Ultimate for its Grain, so Chiz at Awakening 0 suits Beyond the Rails or High-Risk Commissions; after it, she is strong in the open world too. | [9:30](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=570) |
| Tip | Chiz has hardly any comfortable swap-combo points: swapping out during the Ultimate ends its state, and swapping after the first Skill restarts the chain on return; usually finish a full Skill set before swapping. | [11:21](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1yXokBKEj4/?t=681) |
