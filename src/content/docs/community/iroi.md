## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被, 零号攻略组](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | As a support: on entry, use her Ultimate if ready, then her Skill, then swap out. | [1:19](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=79) |
| Reason | A support Iroi skips her charged attack: its wind-up is long, wastes buff time and loses damage if interrupted before the swap; swapping straight out gains more. | [2:49](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=169) |
| Tip | Tap her Skill in normal play: holding it turns allies into lambs and cuts team damage sharply, worth it only for a fully awakened Iroi. | [4:05](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=245) |
| Tip | Playing Iroi on the field, dodge right after her third Basic Attack to cancel its recovery; the damage still lands. | [13:16](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=796) |
| Tip | For a swap combo, use her second Basic Attack: swap as soon as she floats holding the lamb or has just pulled it out. | [13:23](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=803) |
| Tip | Hold Basic Attack during the animation of a dodge counter, Support Skill, Skill or Ultimate, and her charged attack starts straight from its third stage. | [13:43](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=823) |
| Tip | The easy charged-attack swap: after her Skill or Ultimate, hold Basic Attack and swap once she starts shooting with the lamb; reliable, but she stays on the field longer. | [13:56](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=836) |
| Tip | The fast charged-attack swap: hold Basic Attack, tap it once more as the third stage starts (when she jumps), then swap at once; less time on the field, but the timing is strict. | [14:04](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=844) |
| Tip | A charged attack without her Skill: with enough Imagination, hold Basic Attack, tap it twice quickly once her right hand pulls out the lamb, then swap; she finishes the whole sequence. | [14:18](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=858) |
| Tip | A charged attack from 0 Imagination: Skill → Ultimate → hold Basic Attack → tap it as she jumps. The Skill's 16 Imagination keeps the field up for 2 s, and charged attacks inside it cost nothing. | [14:32](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=872) |
| Tip | On the field, three Basic Attacks then a dodge has lower DPS but the best survival and is the most recommended; with Imagination, hold Basic Attack first, about 58% more than plain Basic Attacks in a swap combo. | [15:03](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Hfgm6eE2A/?t=903) |
