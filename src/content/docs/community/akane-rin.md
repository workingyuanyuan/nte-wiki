## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被, 零号攻略组](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | The core loop: Ultimate and Skill on entry, Basic Attacks to fill the energy, then the Enhanced Skill and the second-stage Ultimate once the Skill lights up. | [0:44](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=44) |
| Reason | Use the Ultimate first, because it gives Akane Rin 20% ATK. | [0:52](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=52) |
| Tip | The Skill sets her damage mode: tap it for a single target, hold it for groups. | [1:01](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=61) |
| Tip | When the first set is done, the buffs from her Ultimate, cartridges and Arc are usually still up: Basic Attack again to fill the energy and use a second Enhanced Skill. | [1:23](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=83) |
| Tip | If the order is hard to remember: Ultimate on entry, then press whatever lights up and Basic Attack otherwise; swap out after two Enhanced Skills. | [1:55](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=115) |
| Tip | The second-stage Ultimate can wait a little: it ends her interruption resistance but not her ATK bonus; the guitar on the left of the screen shows the time left, and it can be used until the strings run out. | [10:48](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=648) |

| Tip | A dodge after any Basic Attack hit becomes a slide that goes further with longer invincibility and keeps the combo going; weave it in at any time. | [11:19](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BeJC6qEAr/?t=679) |
