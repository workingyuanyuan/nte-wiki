## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | On her own: use the Skill and Ultimate for their buffs, then keep using Basic Attack; swap out once the ring left of the HP bar is full. | [1:01](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=61) |
| Tip | With Ready-Ready, use the Skill, the Ultimate, then the Arc skill before attacking, for an extra 10% DMG against bosses. | [5:40](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=340) |
| Reason | Use the Skill before the Ultimate so the high-multiplier Ultimate gets the Skill's 30% CRIT DMG bonus. | [9:36](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=576) |
| Tip | The Ultimate lasts only 6 s: five Basic Attacks, then hold for a heavy-attack string, holding as soon as the last hit goes out. | [10:20](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=620) |
| Tip | During the Ultimate only Basic Attacks pull enemies in, so keep using them to keep enemies grouped. | [10:38](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=638) |
| Tip | The first heavy attack has a long wind-up, so chain it after Basic Attacks: hold the button right after the fifth hit. | [10:50](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=650) |
| Tip | The third heavy attack, the uppercut, has a long recovery; dodge-cancel it once its damage lands. | [10:58](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=658) |
| Tip | Swap combo: after her fourth Basic Attack, Nanally finishes it and the fifth on her own, so swap in an ally for a Skill; she takes no damage off the field. | [12:17](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=737) |
| Tip | After her fourth hit, swap to an ally's Ultimate: during its animation she finishes her moves while timers, buffs and cooldowns stand still, giving nearly three Basic Attack strings in the 6 s Ultimate, more with Awakening 2. | [12:36](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=756) |
| Tip | Going further: after the fourth hit, swap to Jiuyuan for a Skill, to Sakiri for a held Skill, then to Esper Zero for the Ultimate; all three act during the time stop. | [13:04](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=784) |
| Tip | Without swap combos, against stunned or harmless enemies: after Skill and Ultimate, five Basic Attacks, a held heavy attack, a dodge-cancel, then five more Basic Attacks or three heavy attacks; this uses the buffs fully and fills the Esper Cycle. | [13:16](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=796) |
| Tip | If Nanally's energy is a worry, attack one more string before swapping out. | [13:37](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ybo8BPEpx/?t=817) |
