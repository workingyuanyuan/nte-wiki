## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被, 零号攻略组](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | Unlock her Enhanced Skill with her Skill rather than the fifth Basic Attack; her Basic Attacks and hold attack at Connection level 1 are rarely needed. | [1:05](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=65) |
| Tip | Basic flow: Skill → Enhanced Skill → three Basic Attacks → Enhanced Skill → Ultimate, then swap out while she keeps attacking off the field; in short, Skill when ready, Basic Attack otherwise, Ultimate and swap at full Connection. | [1:22](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=82) |
| Tip | Raise her Connection to level 3 before her Ultimate: its multiplier is +50% at level 2 and +100% at level 3, single-target or AoE alike. | [11:21](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=681) |
| Tip | Using her Ultimate twice does not bank off-field attacks: a new Ultimate resets the count. | [12:10](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=730) |
| Tip | At full Connection, three Basic Attacks still unlock another second-stage Enhanced Skill, which stops the timer while it plays; in Beyond the Rails, use that to add damage. | [12:57](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=777) |
| Tip | Swap combo: at the second Basic Attack, input the third early and swap to an ally so both animations overlap, combined with the Enhanced Skill's timer stop. | [13:57](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=837) |
| Reason | This kind of swap play does not deal standout damage yet; for now it can be tried with Akane Rin or Zankou. | [14:07](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=847) |
| Tip | A timer-cover team with Zankou, Linko and Sakiri: Zankou does one Basic Attack, charged hold, Basic Attack string each time, alternating with allies; each round needs 10 full strings with RES shred and Hexed kept up throughout. | [14:16](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=856) |
| Tip | In the timer-cover team, Elyms uses her first-stage Enhanced Skill, then two Basic Attacks, taps once more before the second ends, and swaps out so she finishes the third. | [15:14](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=914) |
| Tip | In the timer-cover team, her first-round Ultimate comes before her Connection is full; from the second round she uses it at full Connection and adds one Skill after it, with all else and the round time unchanged. | [15:54](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Czxo6AEBF/?t=954) |
