## Playing tips

|  | [叫我棉被, 零号攻略组](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/) | Video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reason | No need to build her gauge on purpose: Basic Attacks fill it slowly, and its up to 40% bonus sits in the DMG bonus multiplier, where it is easily diluted. | [1:56](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=116) |
| Tip | On entry, use her Ultimate if ready, then keep tapping Skill until the pop-up appears, pick an ally for a Synced Strike, and swap out. | [2:01](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=121) |
| Tip | At Awakening 0, consider picking allies whose Support Skill effects trigger, such as Iroi for healing and Imagination. | [3:40](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=220) |
| Tip | With no ally you particularly want, swap out right after the Skill: Xiaozhen's Synced Strike fires by itself and no damage is lost. | [3:55](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=235) |
| Reason | There is no need to keep Linko on the field for Basic Attacks: they have low multipliers and swap poorly, while her off-field marks are worth a lot. | [6:30](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=390) |
| Tip | After her Skill you can hold the selection without choosing (the timer stops for about 14 s), which works wonders for swap combos with Iroi; Blossom flowers do not attack during the stop. | [15:18](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19rtS6vEg7/?t=918) |
